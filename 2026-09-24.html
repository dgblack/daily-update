Real-world evaluation of low-earth-orbit satellite communication for surgical telementoring: implications for future robotic surgery

Oki E, Kawazoe T, Nakanoko T, et al. · J Robot Surg, 9 Sep 2026 · doi:10.1007/s11701-026-03936-y

The authors tested Starlink links for laparoscopic telementoring over about 15 km (Tokyo), 100 km and 1,000 km in Japan. At 15 km the video showed frequent bitrate swings and freezes. The 100 km link could not stay stable. The 1,000 km link reached 34.4 ms latency at 2.51 Mbps on day 1, but on day 2 latency peaked at 765 ms and 16.7% of bitrate samples fell below 2 Mbps. The authors conclude that current LEO links are not yet reliable enough for clinical telementoring.

Why it matters: real latency and jitter numbers from LEO satellite links, useful as a network model when you design delay-robust tele-ultrasound for remote sites.

Weakly Supervised Spatial Grounding for Discriminative Attention-Based Ultrasound-Histopathology Alignment in Prostate Cancer Grading

Dzikunu, Willis, Abootorabi, Harmanani, Guo, Luger, Kinnaird, Wodlinger, Mousavi, Abolmaesumi (UBC / Queen's / Exact Imaging) · arXiv 2609.15150, 14 Sep 2026

Aligns micro-ultrasound needle-region features with pathology grading. Weak spatial supervision from routine biopsy measurements (the proportion of malignant tissue) guides which patches the attention mechanism focuses on. On 7,166 cores from 811 patients at seven centres, it reaches 67.1 macro AUC and 68.5 csPCa AUC. The unimodal baselines score about 63.

Why it matters: this is the current state of the art for multi-centre micro-US prostate AI from the UBC/Queen's group, and it is complementary to elastography.

Telerobotic surgery is advancing around the world. Will the US embrace it?

Susan Kelly · MedTech Dive, 23 Sep 2026

An overview of where telesurgery stands:

  • About 5,000 remote surgeries have been performed worldwide.
  • SS Innovations reports more than 185 telerobotic procedures.
  • MicroPort received a CE mark for telesurgery in June 2026.
  • ARPA-H has awarded up to $175.3M for remote stroke robots, with Siemens Healthineers and Philips among the recipients.
  • No US surgical robot is yet authorized for remote use, and the FDA is drafting guidance.

The article names licensure, credentialing, reimbursement and latency as the barriers to US adoption.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Fine Wrist Control as a Marker of Surgical Teleoperation Expertise

Gale, Shobayashi, Satpathy, Davidor, Nisky, Okamura · arXiv 2609.27160, 22 Sep 2026

Tracked upper-limb motion of 27 novices and 9 experts during a peg-transfer task on a surgical robot. Experts stabilised the wrist more while keeping enough shoulder and elbow range of motion, and they finished faster. The authors argue that training should target operator biomechanics as well as task outcomes.

MATE: Multi-Agent Virtual Teleoperation Platform for Humanoid Collaboration Data Collection

Yu, Wang, Ren, et al., Yuexin Ma · arXiv 2609.26520, 22 Sep 2026

Geographically distributed operators jointly teleoperate humanoids in a shared physics simulation. The team collected 24.1 h and 2,500 episodes across five collaborative tasks, and policies transferred zero-shot to a physical humanoid. The abstract reports no network or latency figures.

A Deployable Four-Finger Payload for Teleoperated Free-Flying Manipulation with Astrobee

Su, Kam, Nakamura, Wang, Zhou, Tomizuka (Berkeley) · arXiv 2609.25614, 22 Sep 2026

A 17-DoF four-finger gripper controlled by VR hand tracking. Hand pose drives the finger pairs, hand separation moves the linear rail, and wrist motion translates the free-flyer. It was evaluated only in a zero-gravity MuJoCo simulator of the ISS.

A Residual Spatial Attention Graph Network for Sensorless Force Estimation in Teleoperated Robots

Liang K, Cai Y, Feng W, et al. · Ann N Y Acad Sci, Sep 2026 · doi:10.1111/nyas.70388

RSAG-TCN estimates contact forces without a force sensor. It beat six baselines, lowering RMSE by 0.024 N in soft contact and by 0.104 N in hard contact. It was also validated in real time with six operators.

Why it matters: sensorless force estimation could supply haptic feedback in low-cost tele-ultrasound robots.

Impact of Data Compression on Downstream AI Tasks: A Study using Teleoperated Driving over 5G

Zhang, Sleder, Hu, Bilal, Ye, Zhi-Li Zhang (Minnesota) · arXiv 2609.25290, 21 Sep 2026

Camera and LiDAR streams generate hundreds of Mbps, which exceeds typical 5G uplink capacity. The authors measure how lossy compression of video, LiDAR and both combined degrades object recognition and segmentation. They identify an empirical optimum trade-off for the multimodal case. This is a companion to the SHARDED paper reported yesterday.

Under-insured, over-insured, but not properly insured: the structural liability-coverage gap in co-located robotic surgery and telesurgery

Diamond DA, Moschovas MC, Dohler D, et al. · J Robot Surg, 10 Sep 2026 · doi:10.1007/s11701-026-03871-y

Argues that current telesurgery insurance duplicates premiums while leaving gaps around cyber-physical failures and unclear standards of care. The authors propose a single integrated multi-domain liability product.

Computer vision & imaging

Impact of 5-ALA and sodium fluorescein on FLAIR volume extent of resection and survival in IDH-wildtype glioblastoma

Turin / Genova groups · J Neuro-Oncol, 16 Sep 2026 · doi:10.1007/s11060-026-05802-1

A retrospective study of 154 glioblastoma patients: 63 received 5-ALA, 69 sodium fluorescein and 22 both. Extent of resection did not differ significantly, for either the enhancing tumour or the FLAIR region. 5-ALA was associated with longer progression-free survival (8.5 vs 6.0 months) and overall survival (17.0 vs 11.0 months), and the difference held after adjustment. The authors suggest that 5-ALA gives a more biologically selective resection.

Why it matters: clinical context for 5-ALA versus fluorescein as targets for hyperspectral fluorescence quantification.

Open Ultrasound Foundation Model for Robust Segmentation and Clinical Measurement Across Heterogeneous Settings

Chao Qin, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Salman Khan, et al. · arXiv 2609.19230, 16 Sep 2026

SonoBase is an open ultrasound segmentation foundation model trained on 456,963 images from 53 datasets. The authors report ejection-fraction estimates within inter-observer variability (6.63% error). This summary is based on the arXiv listing abstract.

Industry & news

XCath secures FDA breakthrough designation for remote thrombectomy robot

Medical Device Network, 11 Sep 2026

XCath's Iris endovascular robot received FDA Breakthrough Device designation for remote mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. In March 2026 the same system performed a live-patient remote thrombectomy across more than 120 miles in Panama.

Philippines Makes Robotics History: Surgeon Operates on Patient 1,380 KM Away

eWeek, 14 Sep 2026 (procedures on 5 Sep)

The Philippines' first cross-country telesurgeries were two cholecystectomies with the SSI Mantra system, between Pampanga and Cotabato City. The article notes that connectivity reliability remains the main barrier to routine use.

The Apple Vision Pro was used for a successful hip surgery

Engadget, 3 Sep 2026

Stryker's SportSuite Vision app received FDA De Novo authorization in July. It was first used clinically at Duke Health in a hip arthroscopy, showing planning data, scope video and CT beside the patient in the surgeon's field of view.
