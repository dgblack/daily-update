“It wasn't just a robot”: Women's and providers' experiences with telerobotic ultrasound in rural prenatal care

Khan, Prychak, Burbridge, Mendez (Saskatchewan) · Women's Health (Lond), online 1 Oct 2026 · DOI

This mixed-methods study used interviews and surveys with 40 participants (19 patients and 21 providers) in Gravelbourg and Saskatoon. Participants valued telerobotic ultrasound mainly because it meant less travel. The barriers they reported were low awareness, inconsistent communication, knowledge gaps, and gaps in cultural and language accommodation. The authors say successful roll-out needs attention to cultural safety, patient communication, workflow integration and reliable technical infrastructure. The abstract was read via Europe PMC.

Why it matters: this is user-side evidence from the main Canadian telerobotic ultrasound program. Its barriers are mostly about communication and workflow rather than the robot, which is useful when arguing for human-teleoperation alternatives.

Deformable CT-US Registration via Anatomy-Aware Implicit Neural Representations

Lach, Wysocki, Li, Azampour, Killeen, …, Navab (TUM) · arXiv, 6 Oct 2026 · ASMUS 2026 (MICCAI workshop)

The method starts from a rigid registration given by robot-assisted optical tracking. It then optimises a sinusoidal implicit neural representation of the deformation for each frame. Hounsfield values from CT set how stiff each region is, so bone stays rigid while soft tissue can deform. The method also adds a contact-zone displacement model for probe pressure and a regulariser for the fan geometry, and it optimises a normalised-gradient-field similarity. It improves on the rigid initialisation by 17% with little topological distortion.

Why it matters: correcting for deformation caused by probe pressure is directly relevant to registering patient models in robotic and tele-ultrasound.

UltraDiff: Differentiable Ray Tracing in Ultrasound for Shape Optimization

Duelmer, Wysocki, Navab, Azampour (TUM) · arXiv, 6 Oct 2026 · SIGGRAPH Asia 2026

The authors model ultrasound image formation as transient rendering, with echoes arranged by travel time, and implement it in Mitsuba 3. This makes the image formation differentiable with respect to geometry. Using analysis-by-synthesis without segmentation, they recover vertebral surfaces from simulated B-mode sweeps and from real robotic sweeps of spine phantoms, with competitive geometric accuracy.

Why it matters: this is a physics-based alternative to learned 3D ultrasound reconstruction, and it works from tracked robotic sweeps.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

TeleHairing: A Teleoperation Baseline for Robotic Haircutting

Huang, Vilkki, Huang, Liao, Luo, Angrisani, Li · arXiv, 5 Oct 2026

This is a closed-loop system for haircutting on a mannequin with an operator in the loop, tested locally and remotely. Remote latency was about 190.5 ms overall, and most of the increase came before the robot-side control endpoint. Remote command-following RMSE was 45.1 mm, dominated by the final withdrawal phase; without that segment it was 9.6 mm. Motion resumed smoothly after the tracker lost the target.

Why it matters: it is a close analogue of teleoperated contact tasks on a person's body, and it includes a useful breakdown of where the latency comes from.

MRPilot: Supervising and Intervening LLM-Based Multi-Robot Teams through Mixed Reality

Yang, Qian, Zhan, Tran, Liu, Jin · arXiv, 5 Oct 2026

MRPilot is an MR interface for supervising robot teams driven by an LLM. It has four stages: Forming (resolving ambiguous references), Reviewing plans, Following execution, and Repairing. In a VR-simulated study with 20 participants, it reduced workload and improved situational awareness, transparency, trust and sense of control compared with a chat-style interface.

Remote health & communications

Mitigating Concept Drift in QoS Prediction for Teleoperation of Autonomous Vehicles Using Historic Data

Su, Gao, Ashri, Diermeyer (TUM) · arXiv, 6 Oct 2026

The paper forecasts uplink data rate and round-trip latency on mobile networks for teleoperation. It uses historical data to counter concept drift when models meet unfamiliar network conditions, and it proposes “critical scenario detection” as an evaluation metric specific to teleoperation. The abstract gives no numbers.

Why it matters: predicting QoS ahead of time could let a tele-ultrasound system adapt its video or haptic bandwidth before the link degrades.

Industry & news

SRS India 2026: minister frames tele-robotics around imaging and guidance, not remote surgery

Cavalier (current-affairs summary) · 2 Oct 2026

In the inaugural address at SRS India 2026, Dr Jitendra Singh, India's Minister of State for Science and Technology, mentioned an ultrasound performed on a person about 10,000 km away in Antarctica. He argued that fibre latency of about 50 ms each way is tolerable for imaging but not for routine remote surgery. He said near-term tele-robotics should focus on diagnosis, imaging, guidance and proctoring. This is a secondary summary; no primary transcript was found.
