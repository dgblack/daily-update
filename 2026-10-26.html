Top picks

From Freehand Demonstrations to Autonomous Robotic Ultrasound: An Anatomy-Anchored Learning Framework

Yuan Bi, Fangxu Han, Ye Su, Yiping Zhou, Zhongliang Jiang, Nassir Navab · arXiv (cs.RO), 23 Oct 2026

Demonstrations are recorded with a handheld rig during normal freehand scanning, so no robot is needed to collect data. At run time, external cameras find an anatomy-specific starting pose, which becomes the reference frame. A flow-based policy then drives the probe from anchor-relative pose, the US image, a probe-mounted camera and contact force, and a separate head decides when the scan is done.

On 16 unseen participants, success was 96% (kidney), 94% (gallbladder), 88% (liver standard plane) and 94% (radial artery sweep). That is 10–42 points above a fine-tuned π0 on the abdominal tasks, with lower peak force and jerk. Policies transferred to a second robot without retraining (83–100%).

Why it matters: the handheld rig is essentially the follower side of human teleoperation. Freehand tele-US sessions could serve directly as training data for this kind of policy, giving a route from human teleoperation to shared or full autonomy.

Korean doctor operates remotely from Seoul on pig in Shanghai

Jung Min-ho, The Korea Times, 21 Oct 2026

Kang Seok-ho (Korea University urology) operated on a pig in Shanghai from a console at the Korean Urological Association meeting in Seoul, about 870 km away. He used MicroPort MedBot's Toumai system over ordinary telecom networks, with roughly 30–40 ms latency. It is billed as Korea's first cross-border telesurgery on a commercial platform. Kang cites about 1,500 human telesurgeries worldwide, mostly in China, and plans more animal trials, possibly with a single-port robot.

Clinical application analysis of remote robot-assisted sleeve gastrectomy and bariatric anti-reflux surgery (in Chinese, English abstract)

Zhou ZQ, Aili A, …, Abudureyimu K (Xinjiang Medical Univ.; People's Hospital of Xinjiang; Aksu First People's Hospital) · Zhonghua Wei Chang Wai Ke Za Zhi 29(10):1220–1226, Oct 2026 · doi

A retrospective series of 7 patients operated from Urumqi on patients in Aksu between Oct 2025 and Mar 2026. Latency was about 73 ms with packet loss of 0.02–0.06%, and there was no lag or dropout. At 3 months, excess weight loss was 53.5% and GERD-Q scores improved. The abstract does not name the robot platform.

Why it matters: together with the Korea item, this adds to the growing record of routine domestic telesurgery at 30–80 ms. It is a useful reference range when you discuss acceptable latency for tele-US.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

ViT-H: Generative Video-To-Humanoid Control with Motion Tracking Feedback

Zimo He, Tianyi Bian, Zizhong Yang, Hongjie Li, …, Siyuan Huang, Yixin Zhu · arXiv (cs.RO), 23 Oct 2026

A diffusion model generates humanoid reference motion directly from monocular human video, skipping the reconstruct–retarget–track pipeline. It is then post-trained with DPO, using tracking success as the preference signal. An autoregressive variant enables real-time video teleoperation of a Unitree G1 with under 400 ms latency.

DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection

Amber Xie, Jiawei Fu, Xiaofeng Guo, Carmelo Sferrazza, Guanya Shi, Haozhi Qi · arXiv (cs.RO), 23 Oct 2026

A portable device carries the deployment robot hand itself. The operator sets wrist motion by moving the device and picks or adjusts finger "skills" instead of controlling each joint. Over 2,500 demos were collected. Training on recorded commands rather than measured joint states raised success from 11% to 64%, and adding in-the-wild data raised it from 36% to 78%.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Beyond Discrete Tokens: Preserving Numerical Continuity in Medical Vision-Language Models

Dianye Huang, Jiamei Guo, Yue Zhou, Mingde Li, Yi Xiong, Wei Wang, Nassir Navab, Zhongliang Jiang · arXiv (cs.CV), 23 Oct 2026

HAD-VLM adds a diffusion head to a VLM so that caliper endpoints and lengths are predicted as continuous values rather than text tokens. On EchoNet-LVH, the mean absolute errors are 1.16–3.05 mm, 21–38% below an autoregressive baseline and better than fine-tuned Qwen3-VL. The model keeps its medical VQA performance.

Remote health & medical robotics

Robots for CT-guided needle insertion: clinical implementation and future perspectives

Matsui Y, Hiraki T, …, Kamegawa T (Okayama Univ.) · Jpn J Radiol, online 24 Oct 2026 · doi

A review of needle-insertion robots for ablation, biopsy and localization. It includes the group's own remotely operated needle-advancement system, which matched manual accuracy with no radiation exposure to the physician. The authors name AI and telerobotics as next steps.

Emergency undocking in robotic surgery: an international Delphi consensus study

Ryan G, Shakir T, Francis N (UCL; Griffin Institute) · J Robot Surg 20:1135, 26 Oct 2026 · doi

Fifty-two experts from 10 countries agreed on 37 statements over four rounds, 25 of them platform-agnostic. The statements still need simulation and clinical validation. The paper is relevant background for safety protocols when the surgeon is remote and cannot undock.

Computer vision & imaging

OPTARI: An open-source software framework for clinical translation of optoacoustic imaging

Moritz Schillinger, Maximilian Bader, Adrian Buehler, Felix Wachter, Ferdinand Knieling, Vasilis Ntziachristos, Katharina Breininger · arXiv, 23 Oct 2026

A no-code GUI for analysing combined optoacoustic and ultrasound data, with segmentation, spectral unmixing and batch processing. It is already used in ongoing clinical studies. The spectral unmixing tools may be useful for hyperspectral work too.
