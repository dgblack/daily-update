Top picks

Deep Reinforcement Learning Assistance for Resilient Haptic Teleoperation over a 5G Network

Georgios Kokkinis, Aitor Hernandez Herranz, Qi Zhang · arXiv (eess.SP), 21 Oct 2026 · submitted to IEEE TMC

A DRL agent at the network edge sits next to the remote environment, so it sees the environment state without network delay, and blends its actions with the operator’s haptic commands. On an Ericsson 5G standalone testbed under several congestion and QoS settings, assistance helped in every condition. Under congestion it cut mean task completion time by 20.6%. Under congestion, assisted operation without QoS prioritisation also beat unassisted operation with it. The task is a haptic-steered virtual spaceship game, and the abstract gives no absolute latencies.

Why it matters: it is an edge-side alternative to model-mediated or predictive approaches for haptic links over real cellular networks. It is also a useful comparison point for 5G tele-ultrasound latency arguments.

Remote Expert Evaluation of Tabletop Avatar Gaze Control in Asymmetric Multi-Scale MR–VR Collaboration

Carlos Mosquera, Neven Elsayed, Ernst Kruijff, Joseph Newman, Valeriya Lehrbaum, Eduardo Veas · arXiv (cs.HC), 19 Oct 2026 · VRST 2026

A remote expert places and rotates an avatar in a scaled tabletop model of an industrial plant using a tangible interface, and gets an egocentric in-plant view through the avatar. The study compared three gaze-control modes: immersive VR with head control, a floating screen with head control, and an on-avatar screen with a touchpad. With 29 participants on two fault-detection tasks, the immersive VR mode gave the best overall performance and user experience.

Why it matters: the setup is close to the expert side of MR human teleoperation, with an asymmetric MR–VR pairing and remote viewpoint control.

HuMiT: Low-Latency Whole-Body Humanoid Teleoperation via Compact Reference Tracking

Liu Cao, Botian Xu, Ruoqu Chen, et al., Mengdi Xu · arXiv (cs.RO), 21 Oct 2026

The controller tracks a compact reference instead of retargeted full-body motion. The reference is root height, root velocity and four sparse keypoint poses, which avoids retargeting and future-frame conditioning. On a Unitree G1 it reports about 20 ms communication-to-inference latency and 65 ms median end-to-end latency, measured by optical flow.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Warping the Workspace: Expanding Visual Access with Adjustable Reach Mapping for Humanoid Teleoperation

Keigo Minamida, Koya Dendo, Yuto Nakamura, Jun Rekimoto · arXiv (cs.RO/cs.HC), 20 Oct 2026 · UIST 2026

“Coupled visual–motor remapping” pairs a panoramic azimuth warp of the operator’s view with an inverse yaw transform on the arms, so side and rear targets can be reached without turning. In a small study (N=8, 72 trials), Global Zoom completed 23/24, Focus+Context 21/24 and the fixed baseline 20/24. Six of the eight participants still preferred Focus+Context.

Preserving Operator Intent Under Actuation Constraints: Teleoperation of Manipulators with Coupled Transmissions

Xuhan Miao, Zijin Han, Yutong Shen, et al., Chenguang Yang · arXiv (cs.RO), 21 Oct 2026

This leader–follower scheme coordinates motor saturation with gravity compensation so that pitch–roll coordination in differential wrists survives saturation. Real-robot tests show smoother wrist trajectories and better recovery than direct joint mapping. The abstract gives no numbers.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Learning to Measure and Diagnose: A Clinical-Dictionary Radiomic Bottleneck Model for Thyroid Nodule Classification

Guibin Zhang, Weier Luo, Lei Cao · arXiv (cs.CV), 21 Oct 2026

The network estimates clinically defined radiomic measurements from thyroid ultrasound and feeds them to a linear classifier, so each malignancy call can be explained. It is evaluated on ThyroidXL and TN3K, and the abstract reports competitive results without numbers. This was a quiet day for ultrasound otherwise; nothing new on robotic or tele-ultrasound appeared on arXiv or Scholar.
