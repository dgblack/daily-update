(Elastography) E-BI-RADS: A Novel Classification System for Breast Elastography to Enable Safe Biopsy De-escalation

Barr RG, Pfob A, Bauman P, … Golatta M · Invest Radiol, online 12 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43016126

This retrospective study covers 344 lesions in 337 patients, 224 of them malignant. The classification combines shear-wave velocity with the ratio of lesion size on elastography to size on B-mode. It reached 99.6% sensitivity (223/224) and 74.2% specificity, against 98.6% and 34.2% for B-mode BI-RADS. No lesion in E-BI-RADS 2 was malignant, and 94.4% of E-BI-RADS 5 lesions were.

Why it matters: a simple SWE-based rule could be a clinically meaningful output for tele-elastography or robot-acquired elastography.

USB Gym: A Gymnasium Testbed for Closed-Loop Ultrasound View Matching

Intharah T, Gao Z, Liu Y, Dong H · arXiv (cs.CV/cs.RO), 5 Nov 2026

The simulator renders ultrasound from public CT, and the probe-guidance pipeline has three swappable parts: segmenter, controller and reward. The authors ran 20 published methods on the same held-out patients. Only an SSIM reward did detectably better than their own components. The best combined guide moved the probe from 16.0 mm to only 13.7 mm from the target view, while a pose oracle reached 1.5 mm, so the guide made about 16% of the oracle's progress. Nothing was tested on hardware.

Why it matters: this is a sober benchmark for autonomous probe guidance, and it supports keeping a human (teleoperated or guided) in the loop for now.

Metasurface waveguides for all-day augmented reality glasses

Yun JG, Kang H, Choi M, … Rho J · Nature, online 11 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43015478

The prototype glasses use a dual-layer interwoven metasurface waveguide that is 1.5 mm thick. They give a 30° field of view and an 8×8 mm² 2D eyebox, with an average efficiency of 1,938 nits/lm (up to 4,225).

Why it matters: glasses-class AR hardware would make headset-based human teleoperation much easier to wear for a whole clinical shift.

In vivo cardiac biomechanical model parameter estimation from ultrafast shear wave elastography

Ferrario C, Venet M, Villemain O, … Sermesant M · Biomech Model Mechanobiol 25(6):122, 11 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43013103

A reduced-order closed-loop electromechanical model is fitted by CMA-ES to myocardial stiffness waveforms measured with SWE over the cardiac cycle. In 20 healthy children the fitted model's median MAE was 1.19 kPa and its median correlation 0.995. The ATP rate parameter was the least identifiable.

Teleoperation, haptics & mixed reality

Evaluating Tactile Encoding Strategies for Communicating 3-D Shape

Harnett MC, Friesen RF · arXiv, 10 Nov 2026

The study compares one tactile height map with a stack of binary slices for presenting 3D shape on 2D haptic displays, using a pin array and a friction-modulating surface. Participants did a 3D assembly task accurately with both encodings, and the pin array was faster, though not significantly.

Feel My Focus: Gaze-Triggered Digital Twin Creation for Remote Collaboration

Ma S-Y, Weng SC-C, Pai M-Y, et al. · arXiv, 8 Nov 2026

When the local user holds their gaze on an object, the system captures, segments and reconstructs it in 3D in one shot, and the remote partner can then inspect it. The paper reports only qualitative observations from a public demo.

Why it matters: gaze-triggered capture is a lightweight way to share 3D context from the novice's side in MR-guided remote procedures.

ROS2MR: Connecting ROS 2 Motion Planning to Mixed-Reality Trajectory Visualization

Berrezueta-Guzman S, Khalil H-L, Koshelev A, et al. · arXiv, 9 Nov 2026

The tool converts ROS 2 joint trajectories into MR playback files resampled at 30 Hz. Position RMSE is about 3×10-5° when velocities are supplied, but rises to 0.85° for sparse input without them.

Remote health & communications

Nurse-led inferior vena cava assessment using handheld ultrasound within a cardiologist-supported home-care workflow

Suzuki Y, Oohashi F, Ogawa M, … Matsumoto M · Drug Discov Ther, online 12 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43014748

In this case report, home-care nurses acquired IVC images on a handheld device and a cardiologist reviewed them remotely. The patient was 95, with HFpEF and dementia. Over 3 weeks, maximum IVC diameter fell from 19.3 to 11.0 mm and collapsibility rose from 29% to 54.5%. The authors call the workflow feasible but say its clinical utility is unproven.

Why it matters: this is the asynchronous, store-and-forward alternative that real-time tele-guided ultrasound competes with in home care.

Computer vision & imaging

Overcoming Dynamic Range Limitations in Computational Hyperspectral Imaging

Yu Q, Lin J, Yao Z, et al. · arXiv, 10 Nov 2026

The authors fuse multiple exposures with a physics-guided method on a 9-channel computational hyperspectral camera. They measured a 16.0 dB gain in spectral-reconstruction dynamic range over the best single exposure.

Why it matters: limited dynamic range is a practical problem for intraoperative HSI and fluorescence when specular highlights and dim tissue are in the same field.

SurgTAP-3D: A Large-Scale Cross-Surgery Video Dataset with Contact-Aware 3D Motion Tracking

Guo J, Dong W, Wu T, et al. · arXiv, 8 Nov 2026

The dataset has 520K frames, 4,549 action clips and 1.4M 2D/3D point trajectories from robotic and open surgery. An annotation pipeline produces 4D labels from uncalibrated monocular video.
