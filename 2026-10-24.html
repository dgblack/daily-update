Diagnostic performance of micro-ultrasound versus multiparametric MRI for clinically significant prostate cancer in active surveillance: a diagnostic accuracy meta-analysis

E. Barrera-Juárez, R. Rodriguez-Monrroy, A. González-Medina, et al. · Revista Mexicana de Urología 86(4), published online 19 Oct 2026

A PRISMA-DTA meta-analysis of 5 studies (339 men on active surveillance). Pooled sensitivity for csPCa was 96.2% for micro-US and 87.8% for mpMRI. Specificity was low for both, at 41.6% and 47.3%. AUC was 0.84 vs 0.79, a difference that was not significant (p = 0.73). Two of the five studies were conference abstracts only, and risk of bias was moderate to serious.

Why it matters: it adds to the case for micro-US as an accessible alternative to MRI. The low specificity is the opening for elastography and other tissue-property add-ons.

V-JEPA Hand: Simple, Accurate and Fast 3D Hand and Camera Trajectory Recovery from Egocentric Videos

Ruicheng Li, Qixiu Li, Yu Deng, Sicheng Xu, Jiaolong Yang, Baining Guo · arXiv (cs.CV), 19 Oct 2026

A single feed-forward model on a V-JEPA 2.1 encoder recovers per-frame two-hand poses, hand presence and camera egomotion in one pass. Hand trajectories come out in a common world frame. It reports the lowest coverage-aware MPJPE among compared methods on ARCTIC, HOT3D and EgoStandard, and lower world-frame trajectory error than SLAM-based pipelines. It runs at 93.4 FPS on one RTX A6000.

Why it matters: fast hand tracking in the world frame from a head-worn camera could track the follower's hand and probe in human teleoperation, or capture expert demonstrations from MR headset video.

Bridge Neurotech partners with Butterfly Network to develop a non-invasive brain-computer interface

Digital Health News · announced 6 Oct 2026, page updated 7 Oct (older than the usual window, but not previously reported)

Bridge Neurotech is a 2026 startup that has raised $13.5M from investors including General Catalyst. It is building a functional ultrasound imaging (fUSI) platform with AI neural decoding. Under a multi-year R&D agreement, it licenses Butterfly's Ultrasound-on-Chip through the Butterfly Embedded program. The deal includes milestone payments and commitments to buy Butterfly hardware. The article also notes a separate Butterfly collaboration with Merge Labs on ultrasound BCIs.

Why it matters: Butterfly Embedded is turning into a platform that other companies license and build on, as with the Mendaera deal reported earlier. Butterfly reports Q3 results on 29 Oct.
