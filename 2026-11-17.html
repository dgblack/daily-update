Adaptive Motion Scaling With Phase-and-Gaze Awareness for Laparoscopic Robotic Teleoperation

Liu J, Cai T, Zhai Y, Luo Y, Wang Z, Guo Y · Advanced Robotics Research, 16 Nov 2026

The framework recognises the surgical phase in real time and combines robot kinematics with eye-tracking features to set the master–slave motion scale. Bayesian optimisation personalises the parameters for each user. Scaling stays steady during fine manipulation and responsive during coarse moves. In a pilot within-subject study with eight non-clinical participants, it reduced clutching and task time and was rated better than fixed and other adaptive scaling methods.

Why it matters: gaze and task-phase cues for adapting the operator mapping could carry over to scaling and guidance in tele-ultrasound.

How Much Does Saliency Accuracy Matter for Saliency-Guided 360-degree Image Compression?

Ahmed S, Senouci S-M, Brunet P, Caron G, Imamoglu N · arXiv, 16 Nov 2026 (accepted to IEEE/SICE SII 2027)

The authors tested saliency predictors spanning about 100× in compute, with both a learned codec and H.265/HEVC. A lightweight predictor running at 52 fps captures 65% of the bitrate saving that ground-truth saliency gives. A model about 3.3× heavier reaches 74%, and a further 38× more compute adds nothing measurable. The stated motivation is low-latency immersive streaming for robot teleoperation.

Why it matters: it gives a practical way to size the saliency model for bandwidth-limited panoramic video in remote MR setups.

Robot Demonstration Videos Should Include Transparency Disclosures

Nakajima N · arXiv cs.RO, 13 Nov 2026

This was a pre-registered experiment with 9,341 participants in six countries. People struggled to tell real footage from CGI, and autonomous robots from teleoperated ones. As a result they overestimated what robots could do. Simple disclosures significantly improved their judgements, and the author argues for disclosure standards.

Why it matters: it is relevant to how teleoperated and human-teleoperation demos are presented to the public and to funders.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

WinDEX: Metric 3D Hand Motion Reconstruction in the Wild for Dexterous Teleoperation and Policy Learning

Ma H, Qu H, … Yin P · arXiv, 16 Nov 2026

A low-cost stereo-inertial head rig and fisheye network reconstruct metric 3D hand motion in the world frame. The training set has about 1,000 hours of annotations. The causal streaming model runs at 171 fps with 37.3 mm world-frame joint error, and the authors demonstrate real-time arm–hand teleoperation.

AVD: A Multi-Sensor Teleoperated Wheelchair Platform with Safety-Aware Control

Chandra N, Thareja M, George B · arXiv, 16 Nov 2026

This low-cost teleoperated wheelchair uses a fixed authority hierarchy: the occupant's joystick and an emergency stop override remote commands, and onboard sensing can only stop the chair. A heartbeat triggers a safe state when the link fails. The authors also measured LiDAR degradation in mist, dust and occlusion. The abstract gives no network latency figures.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Effects of operator experience and measurement depth on skeletal muscle shear wave elastography

Ota T, Yoshida H, Yoneda M · Journal of Ultrasound, online 16 Nov 2026

Two operators with different levels of experience scanned four muscles in 20 healthy adults. Inter-rater reliability was moderate to excellent, with no Bland–Altman bias. Shear-wave velocity varied with depth within the ROI in a muscle-specific way: it was higher deep in the biceps and triceps and lower deep in the rectus femoris.

Why it matters: the operator-dependence and depth effects bear on SWE acquired by novice or remotely guided operators.

Drift-Conditioned Structural Replay for Continual Cardiac Ultrasound Segmentation

Ullah Z, … Kim J · arXiv, 15 Nov 2026

This replay loss for continual learning sets how strongly anatomy is preserved according to how far the model has drifted. The authors report better final segmentation and boundary preservation, with some trade-off in knowledge transfer.
