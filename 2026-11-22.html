From teleoperation to autonomous assistance in robotic acute care surgery: a narrative review of capability, shared control, and the evidence gap

Girishan Prabhu V, Kalatzis A, Azevedo R, … Rohatgi N · J Robot Surg, epub 21 Nov 2026 · DOI

Of 49 surgical robots cleared in the US between 2015 and 2023, 86% offer only assistance, 8% perform discrete tasks and 6% reach conditional autonomy. None of the cleared autonomous functions performs abdominal dissection. The authors argue that acute-care deployment needs autonomy that can handle emergencies, plus a shared-control interface. (Yesterday this was under Other Links with no abstract.)

Why it matters: it is a current, citable figure on regulated autonomy levels, useful for framing shared-control and teleoperation work.

Ultrasound-Guided Model Predictive Control for Robot-Assisted Needle Steering in Muscle-Contained Double-Layered Tissue

Liu W, Li J, Yang Z · Int J Med Robot Comput Assist Surg, Crossref record 21 Nov 2026

An MPC controller steers a needle using trajectories reconstructed from ultrasound, and it was tested in layered muscle–liver tissue. The abstract claims sub-millimetre accuracy, but the number is garbled in the Crossref record, so I cannot report it. (Summary based on the Crossref abstract.)

Decoupling functional morbidity from oncologic burden through fluorescence-guided thyroid cancer surgery

Melcarne R, Lizarazo JLC, Biffoni M, … Donatini G · Updates Surg, epub 20 Nov 2026 · DOI

This retrospective study compared conventional thyroid surgery at one centre (n = 466) with NIRAF/ICG-guided surgery at another (n = 133). Permanent hypoparathyroidism was 8.8% versus 0%. Immediate hypoparathyroidism was 42.5% versus 18.1%, and transient hypoparathyroidism was 32.2% versus 11.3% (all p < 0.001). The two arms come from different centres, which limits the comparison.

Why it matters: it adds outcome evidence for fluorescence guidance in a second surgical field, next to your 5-ALA and hyperspectral work.

Imaging & surgical guidance

Multicentre assessment of interactive 3D holograms for enhanced preoperative planning for colorectal liver metastasis resection

Kron P, Kowal MR, Ibrahim M, … Lodge P · Eur J Surg Oncol, epub 17 Nov 2026 · DOI

46 surgeons from 21 centres in 9 countries reviewed 8 cases. Seeing the holograms changed their resectability judgement in 20% of reviews and changed the choice between neoadjuvant treatment and surgery in 21%. 96% of surgeons found the system easy to use.

Quantitative membranous septum area assessment with advanced three-dimensional CT visualization with a mixed reality-capable device

O'Sullivan C, Tsai TY, Lee HW, … Serruys PW · Eur Heart J Digit Health, epub 22 Oct 2026 (PubMed entry this week) · DOI

Measurements from the mixed-reality software agreed with the 3mensio benchmark (ICC > 0.99) across CT series from aortic-stenosis, aortic-regurgitation and coronary-disease cohorts. Two authors work for the company that makes the software (CarnaLife Holo).
