An Open-Source ROS 2 Framework for Shared Control in Surgical Teleoperation: Integrating the Haply Inverse3 with the da Vinci Research Kit

Varga B, Fritz S, Kiss G, Takacs K, Galambos P · arXiv cs.RO, 19 Nov 2026

This is a ROS 2-native bridge from the low-cost 3-DOF Haply Inverse3 to the dVRK. Its translational input is combined with separately tracked VerseGrip orientation to produce 6-DOF commands. In a within-subjects simulated hot-wire task, linear virtual-fixture guidance significantly reduced wire contacts and completion time compared with unassisted control. Progressive-stiffness guidance showed non-significant trends in the same direction. The stack was also run on physical dVRK hardware.

Why it matters: it is open-source Haply-based shared-control infrastructure you could reuse for low-cost haptic master consoles.

PUPPET: Physical-Unified Platform for Presence, Embodiment, and Teleoperation

Vats M, Groshev M, Gomez Cubero C, Castelló Ferrer E · arXiv cs.RO, 19 Nov 2026 (submitted to ICRA 2027)

PUPPET gives remote access to a physical robotics lab through a continuously synchronised VR replica of the robot and task objects. In a within-subject study, 30 participants did an object-transfer task three ways: in person, by 2D teleoperation, and through PUPPET. After learning, VR cut completion time by 27.7% compared with 2D (68.7 s vs 95.0 s), which was statistically indistinguishable from in person (72.1 s). VR also gave more validation-ready trials (85.6% vs 66.0%).

Why it matters: it adds evidence that a synchronised VR replica can match in-person performance, which supports MR interfaces for remote work.

Experimental Validation of a Dominance-Based Trilateral Teleoperation Platform for Cooperative Haptic Guidance

Martinez-Bautista E, Ramirez-Zamora JD, Garcia-Valdovinos LG, … Puerto-Piña AK · Robotics (MDPI), Crossref record 19 Nov 2026

Two commercial haptic masters share control of one slave robot through a dominance factor. Three standard controllers were tested at five dominance settings, in simulation and on hardware. The platform stayed stable, and PD control gave the lowest tracking error. (Summary based on the Crossref abstract.)

Why it matters: dual-master shared authority suits expert-trainee mentoring, which is close to your human-teleoperation tele-ultrasound setting.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

TinyARM-X: A Series of Open-Source, Configurable, and Transparent Torque-Controlled Desktop-Scale Robotic Manipulators

Kosanovic N, Chagas Vaz J · arXiv cs.RO, 18 Nov 2026

TinyARM-X is a family of reconfigurable, torque-controllable desktop manipulators that cost under US$350. Actuator modelling and dynamic compensation support mixed-reality impedance-control teleoperation and force-feedback rendering. The 7-DOF version has a 33.5 g payload and needs 0.7 N to backdrive while moving. The design is open source.

Immersive XR Humanoid Teleoperation for Manipulation and Replay

Guimarães M, Barros G, Teles J, … de Oliveira B · SVR 2026 extended proceedings, Crossref record 19 Nov 2026

This is a Unity client for Meta Quest 3 that drives a humanoid digital twin for pick-and-place tasks. Sessions are recorded for replay and for building imitation-learning datasets. (Summary based on the Crossref abstract.)

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Multimodal In-Process Risk Grading for Robot-Assisted Ultrasound-Guided Thyroid Nodule Puncture: A Phantom Study

Guo Y, … Wang Y · Med Eng Phys, epub 19 Nov 2026 · DOI

The method fuses force sensing, end-effector depth and ultrasound images to grade puncture risk while the procedure is under way. An XGBoost four-level model trained on 536 windows from 100 phantom trials reached accuracy 0.73 and macro AUC 0.90. The authors call it preliminary, phantom-only evidence.

Imaging & surgical guidance

Three-dimensional guidance and AI-assisted navigation in robot-assisted partial nephrectomy: a function-based scoping review

Liu CY, Zhao JD, Sun MZ, … Zhao HW · J Robot Surg 20:1233, 20 Nov 2026 · DOI

The review covers 122 reports, 78 of them clinical. Of the clinical reports, 58 described planning or display without verified spatial registration. Two mixed-reality RCTs found shorter warm ischaemia but no proven renal-function benefit, and no completed trial isolated sustained AI navigation.

Why it matters: it is a useful citation for the claim that most MR guidance lacks verified registration.

Robotic-assisted prostate and kidney surgery: evolution, outcomes, and future directions

Amparore D, Turcan A, … Porpiglia F · Curr Opin Urol, epub 20 Nov 2026 · DOI

This narrative review notes that randomised trials are testing AR guidance in radical prostatectomy and that 3D models are being used for perfusion-aware clamping. It also argues that randomised evidence has moved telesurgery past the feasibility stage.
