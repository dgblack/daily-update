Task-Aware Bitrate Thresholds for Codec-Robust Ultrasound Segmentation

Siyavashi A, Shirzad A, Herglotz C · arXiv, 4 Nov 2026

The authors compressed prostate ultrasound with H.264, HEVC and AV1 at matched perceptual quality, then ran segmentation on the decoded video. All three codecs kept mean Dice at 0.84 or higher down to about 0.07–0.20 bits per voxel. To match AV1, H.264 needed 82% more bitrate for equal Dice and 104.7% more for equal HD95.

Why it matters: it gives task-based bitrate floors for streaming tele-ultrasound to a remote expert or AI model over constrained links, and makes a case for AV1 in that pipeline.

3D ultrasound shear wave elastography quantifies myocardial stiffness and anisotropy in the human heart

Meki T, Pedreira O, Zaroui A, … Papadacci C, Pernot M · Phys Med Biol, online 4 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42981712

The system uses ultrafast volumetric imaging for 3D shear-wave elastography. It was validated against 2D SWE on calibrated phantoms and ex vivo porcine hearts. In vivo, it measured shear-wave speed along and across the fibres in healthy volunteers and in patients with transthyretin cardiac amyloidosis. Amyloid myocardium was stiffer and had lower fractional anisotropy. The abstract gives no numbers.

Why it matters: volumetric SWE with anisotropy is the 3D counterpart of the prostate SWE and micro-US elastography work.

Thermopneumatic Pixels: Fast, Localized, Robust, Low-Voltage Touch Feedback

Linnander M, Visell Y · IEEE Trans Haptics, online 3 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42976055

Each pixel is a thin layered device with no moving parts. Brief electrical pulses heat it and produce transient air pressure. At about 10 V, a pixel under 3 mm thick gives 1 mm displacement and more than 1 N of force within milliseconds, and it can pulse at up to about 300 Hz. Arrays use 4 mm spacing. The paper covers thermal behaviour, durability over tens of thousands of cycles, and perception tests of localization and intensity.

A Multimodal Haptic Interface for Simultaneous Softness and Thermal Modulation

Brown J, Bello F, Jones L · arXiv, 5 Nov 2026

A particle-jamming tactile display circulates heated or cooled water, so it can change stiffness and temperature at the same time. Stiffness rises by up to 300%, temperature spans 10–50 °C, and closed-loop control holds within 0.2 °C of setpoint.

Why it matters: a display that renders softness and temperature together could convey remote tissue properties during palpation, in tele-examination or model-mediated teleoperation.

Teleoperation, haptics & mixed reality

MESSPa: A Multi-modal Embodiment Stimulation Shoulder Pad for Preserving the Sense of Agency in a Detachable Robotic Arm

Vimolmongkolporn V, et al. · arXiv, 31 Oct 2026

A shoulder pad combines one-to-one motion transfer with pneumatic haptic feedback to control a robotic arm that can be detached from the body. Compared with keyboard control, it significantly cut completion time and improved sense of agency in the detached setting. Sense of ownership did not change significantly.

Teleoperation System for Service Robots using Virtual Reality Headset and Pose Estimation

Fernandes E, et al. · arXiv, 1 Nov 2026

MediaPipe pose estimation on RGB-D data from a RealSense D455 maps a VR operator’s arm motion onto the CHARMIE service robot. The authors say wrist yaw capture and precise human-to-robot mapping remain open problems.

Rendering Dynamic Load Cues in Interactive Graphics Via Structured Fingertip Vibration

Vo A, Park J · IEEE Comput Graph Appl, online 5 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42986731

Instead of simulating force, the method conveys dynamic load in VR through vibration patterns spread across the fingertips. The patterns change with how the virtual object is lifted, tilted and rotated. Users rated it more natural and more visuo-haptically congruent, but the abstract gives no numbers.

Precision Through Play: Enhancing Fundamental Laparoscopic Skill Acquisition Via Gamified XR and Pseudo-Haptics

Koohestani B, Rezaei A, Bastanfard A · arXiv, 31 Oct 2026

A gamified XR laparoscopy trainer gives pseudo-haptic feedback when the instrument angle goes out of tolerance. In preliminary tests, precision improved and angular variance fell, but no figures are given.
