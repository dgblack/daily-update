Development of a Blended Variable Impedance-Admittance Controller to Enhance Interaction Transparency and an Image-Based Control for Tele-Ultrasound Examinations

Matilda Ferri (MSc thesis, Univ. of Pisa, Robotics & Automation). Supervisors C.A. Avizzano and A. Filippeschi, tutor F. Porcini. Exam session from 28 Sep 2026. A second thesis with the same title is by Sabrina Di Giuseppe.

The thesis compares constant- and variable-gain impedance and admittance control for the patient-side robot in tele-ultrasound. It then blends the two, weighting each by the estimated environment stiffness, and proves stability with a Lyapunov function. A second part classifies US image quality with ResNet18+SVM, a small CNN and a compact ViT for image-based control. Everything is validated on a teleoperation setup. The full text is embargoed, so only the abstract is available.

Why it matters: this is the same Pisa group (Filippeschi, Frisoli) behind the TDPA delayed renal tele-ultrasound and the redundant-robot work reported earlier. Their tele-US line is growing, and stiffness-dependent blending is directly comparable to your transparency and passivity work.

Variable Admittance Control of Symmetric Teleoperated System for Percutaneous Surgery With Reliable Interaction Force Perception

H. Li, L. Li, Z. Cai (Shanghai Jiao Tong), R. Wang (Inner Mongolia People's Hospital), E. Magid (Kazan Federal) · Int J Med Robot 22(5):e70243, Oct 2026 · doi:10.1002/rcs.70243

The system uses a symmetric master and slave for needle insertion. An adaptive variable admittance controller estimates operator intent from the time derivative of the applied force, with the goal of intuitive motion and reliable force perception at the master. The abstract claims smoother, more precise motion and better safety but gives no numbers.

A Head Camera Is (Almost) All You Need: An Electronics-Free UMI Glove Labelled and Observed by One Moving Camera

Yosub Shin, Yuan Liu, Igor Molybog · arXiv (cs.RO), 20 Oct 2026

A US$122 head-worn camera reads printed fiducials on a US$22 electronics-free glove, so the policy's own video gives 6-DoF gripper pose and jaw width. Median error against robot encoders is 6.5 mm and 1.6°. At deployment the camera sits on a cheap arm that mirrors the collector's head. Adding 194 glove demos to 48 teleop episodes raised success from 10/25 to 17/25, close to the 18/25 from 172 teleop episodes. Head-camera policies also beat wrist and fixed cameras (20/25 vs 15/25 and 12/25).

Why it matters: it shows that one egocentric camera with fiducials is enough to label hand-held tool pose. That is relevant to tracking the follower's probe from an MR headset in human teleoperation.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Extended Reality, Telementoring, and Artificial Intelligence-Assisted Assessment in Surgical Training: A Narrative Review

E. Bademkıran, P. Bademkıran · J Surg Educ 83(12):104208, online 23 Oct 2026 · doi:10.1016/j.jsurg.2026.104208

A structured review that synthesises 50 of 8,113 records from 2014 to May 2026. VR has the strongest evidence, with shorter operative times and fewer errors in trials. AR evidence is mostly orthopaedic and spinal navigation. Telementoring studies mostly involve practising surgeons rather than residents. AI assessment agrees with experts 65–77% of the time. The main gaps it names are implementation and patient-level outcomes.

Computer vision & imaging

Diffusion Posterior Sampling with Uncertain Forward Models

Wentao Shangguan, Vivek K Goyal · arXiv (cs.CV), 21 Oct 2026

The paper proposes an uncertainty-aware Gaussian likelihood that marginalises over uncertain forward-model parameters. It drops into existing diffusion samplers without changing them. Tests cover six inverse problems, including ultrasound. The abstract does not say which ultrasound parameter is uncertain (for example, speed of sound).

Modulating 5-ALA-mediated PpIX accumulation in human glioblastoma cell lines: towards a theranostic strategy

Elijah Abakah (MSc thesis, Univ. of Padua, Pharmaceutical Chemistry; supervisor L. Menilli) · listed on Scholar 5 days ago

The thesis tests metabolic-pathway inhibitors that increase PpIX accumulation in GBM cell lines, aiming to improve margin fluorescence and intraoperative PDT. The work is in vitro only.
