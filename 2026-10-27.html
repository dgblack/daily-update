Doctor performs surgery on patient at hospital 35 miles away using robotics

Katie Kindelan, ABC News / GMA, 26 Oct 2026

On Sun 25 Oct, Vipul Patel of AdventHealth Celebration (Global Robotics Institute) removed the prostate of a 66-year-old man at AdventHealth Minneola, 35 miles away. A qualified surgeon was in the room with the patient, and the patient is reported to be doing well. ABC describes it as the first such surgery between two US hospitals. It was done under an FDA-authorized clinical study, so the system is not cleared for this use. The article names neither the robot platform nor the latency.

Why it matters: this is the first domestic US inter-hospital case under an IDE, after the same team's Florida–Angola trial in 2025. It could become the regulatory template that tele-US robots follow in the US.

Revisiting Direct Control: A Field Evaluation of Sensor Fusion Efficacy in Teleoperating Full-Scale Autonomous Vehicles via Bird's Eye View

Stanisław Kuczma, Sławomir K. Tadeja, Michał R. Nowicki · arXiv (cs.RO), 26 Oct 2026 · accepted at IROS 2026

In a field study, 18 participants directly teleoperated a real car-sized vehicle using one of three views: camera only, a LiDAR bird's-eye view (BEV), or a fused LiDAR and camera BEV. The fused BEV scored best on usability (SUS 77.9) and had the lowest workload (NASA-TLX 34.6, below both alternatives). It also gave the shortest completion time.

Why it matters: it is real-world evidence that a fused, rendered view beats raw video for direct control. That supports the case for MR or reconstructed-scene displays over plain video feeds in tele-US.

EL-DIP: A Deep Image Prior Framework for Accurate, Efficient, and Noise-Robust Elastography

Hao Liu, Fuyi Li, Dong Liu, Yue Mei, Sheng Mao · arXiv (eess.IV), 22 Oct 2026

The method reconstructs heterogeneous shear-modulus maps from measured displacements with a deep image prior, so it needs no training data. Inverse-distance-weighted interpolation handles arbitrarily shaped domains, and adjoint sensitivities give the gradients. Under extreme noise, the authors report about 97% better image fidelity than conventional inversion. The abstract does not say whether the "experimental" data are phantom, ex vivo or in vivo.

Why it matters: because it needs no training data, it could serve as a drop-in alternative for noisy prostate or tele-acquired displacement fields.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Multimodal Socially Aware Framework for Human-Robot Interaction in Virtual Reality

Richard Xu, Dakarai Crowder, Alexis E. Block, Wenzhen Yuan · arXiv, 25 Oct 2026

An autonomous framework combines speech, vision and touch to generate a social robot's speech and movement. In a VR study (N = 18), participants found it acceptable but rated it significantly below a human-teleoperated robot, mainly because of conversational timing and spatial coordination. It is a reminder of how much a teleoperating human contributes to the social side of interaction.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Semantic-Aware Deformable Feature Alignment for Thyroid Nodule Segmentation in Ultrasound

Lei Zhang, Ruhuan Zhou, …, Qin Wang · arXiv, 25 Oct 2026

SDFA-Net separates global semantic learning from local boundary refinement and reports state-of-the-art results on TN3K, TUCC and DDTI. It is incremental, and of interest mainly if you need a thyroid segmentation baseline.

Remote health & medical robotics

Exploring perspectives and engagement in space surgery: a survey of SAGES members

Guidolin K, Srinivas S, Almand A, Hong S, Lim R, Wisbach G, Carroll D, Fer D · Surg Endosc, online 22 Oct 2026 · PubMed

The SAGES space-surgery task force surveyed its members and got a 6.1% response rate. Interest centred on robotic technology. Respondents named remote surgery, microgravity, limited resources and training as the main challenges. One author is an Intuitive employee.

Telesurgery Is Moving from Possibility to Practice

Jennifer Bagley, ACS Clinical Congress 2026 News, 29 Sep 2026

This is a preview of the Clinical Congress panel PS324, "Telesurgery: The Next Frontier Is Here", moderated by J. Redan (AdventHealth) and C. Schlachta (LHSC, Ontario). It recaps the 2025 Florida–Angola IDE trial (12 procedures). It also lists the open issues of credentialing, licensure, consent, cybersecurity, reimbursement and onsite-team skills, and points to ACS/SAGES/SRS checklists. It is useful background for the AdventHealth case above.

Computer vision & imaging

Hyperspectral Unmixing using a Structured Linear Mixing Model with Additive Spectral Variability

Salah Eddine Brezini, Yannick Deville, Moussa Sofiane Karoui, Fatima Zohra Benhalouche, Abdelaziz Ouamri · arXiv, 26 Oct 2026

The paper presents two NMF-based unmixing algorithms built on a linear mixing model with structured additive terms for endmember variability. They outperform existing methods on synthetic and real (remote-sensing) data.

Why it matters: spectral variability is the same problem that PpIX photostate shifts and autofluorescence cause in 5-ALA fluorescence unmixing.
