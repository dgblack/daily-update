Top picks

TEAR: Task-Evidence Authority Routing for Robotic Manipulation

Shicheng Zhang, Hui Liu, Haoliang Li · arXiv (cs.RO), 22 Oct 2026

The paper treats the choice between human teleoperation and local autonomy as an authority-routing problem. A lightweight temporal arbitrator on the robot side times the handoffs. It uses network-state evidence (link quality), task-semantic evidence, robot state and the current authority. In simulation and real-world tests under changing network conditions, success came close to pure teleoperation while the human controlled only 19.8–27.4% of control steps. That was 30–40% higher success than autonomy alone.

Why it matters: it is another way to deal with poor links besides model-mediated teleoperation. Instead of predicting the remote side, the system hands control to local autonomy when the network degrades.

PATH: Preference-Aware Traversability Estimation for Robotic Teleoperation in Off-Road Navigation

Xianghao Meng, Wenshuo Wang, Cheng Shao, et al., Junqiang Xi · arXiv (cs.RO/cs.HC), 22 Oct 2026

PATH-Net produces traversability cost maps and speed recommendations conditioned on how aggressive or conservative the operator is. It is trained with maximum-entropy IRL plus sparse speed supervision. Across 15 operators and 225 teleoperated trajectories, its trajectories matched the operators’ within a modified Hausdorff distance of 0.15–0.17 m. NASA-TLX workload dropped from 34.7 to 22.2 with the recommendations.

Why it matters: the operator-adaptive guidance idea also applies to guidance cues for novice operators in human teleoperation.

XoNav: An Exoskeleton as a Personalized Haptic Interface for Assisted Navigation

Debadrata Sarkar, Soumen Sen, Aman Arora · arXiv (cs.RO), 19 Oct 2026

A four-actuator hip exoskeleton gives advance, braking and steering cues at the joints. A bilateral LSTM gait classifier on two thigh IMUs (95–98% recall on unseen users) gates the cues, and constrained human-in-the-loop Bayesian optimisation tunes them per user. In blindfolded trials, participants kept 98.8% of the distance they walked inside a 0.75 m passage.

Why it matters: it is a worked example of guiding a person through body-grounded haptic cues, which is relevant to how a human follower is guided in human teleoperation.

Ultrasound & imaging

Pos4CHD: A Positive-Free Mask-Guided Counterfactual Synthesis Framework for Fetal Congenital Heart Defect Analysis

Wenjie Xuan, Yapeng Li, Hang Su, et al., Bo Du · arXiv (cs.CV), 22 Oct 2026 · under review at IEEE TMI

Starting from normal cases only, the method edits semantic masks to simulate structural defects, then renders pseudo-positive fetal echo images with a mask-guided generator (FE-Gen). It reports a 12.97-point FID improvement, 93.25% zero-shot accuracy on EbA, +6.95% mAP50 for VSD detection and +10.49% DSC for heart segmentation. Code is public.

AcoustiPrint: Physics-Guided Neural Design of Manufacturable Acoustic Structures

Shanmuka Sadhu, Harshvardhan C. Takawale, Yehia Zakaria, et al. · arXiv, 21 Oct 2026

The method designs 3D-printable acoustic lenses for personalised transcranial focused ultrasound. It reports better focal accuracy than density-based and phase-based baselines, though the abstract gives no numbers. This is peripheral to your work.
