Top picks

YOCO: You Only Calibrate Once! Fast Mocap Calibration for Dexterous Teleoperation

Zhang, Li, Du, Ma, Yang · arXiv (cs.RO), 8 Oct 2026 · CoRL 2026

Low-cost mocap gloves and markerless trackers give hand poses with biases that change with the user, the glove fit and the session. In YOCO, a hypernetwork takes a few paired raw and target poses and predicts LoRA-style updates for a frozen MANO hand estimator. This turns per-user calibration into a single forward pass, with no fine-tuning. It is trained with synthetic drift on InterHand2.6M and tested on real glove recordings and dexterous teleoperation tasks. The authors report gains over uncalibrated input and standard calibration baselines, but the abstract gives no numbers.

Why it matters: fast per-operator calibration of the hand-tracking input is the same problem MR-headset hand tracking poses for human teleoperation.

ZAGNet: Zone-Aware Graph Aggregation Network for Patient-Level Lung Ultrasound Diagnosis

Chen, Patil, Al Mukaddim, Kruecker, Raju, Chen (author list matches Philips Research) · arXiv (eess.IV), 1 Oct 2026 · IEEE IUS 2026

Findings from each lung zone become graph nodes linked by anatomical adjacency. A graph transformer with a virtual global node then predicts consolidation and pleural effusion for the whole patient. It needs only patient-level labels and tolerates missing zones. On 714 subjects and 20,256 video loops from several centres, it reached AUC 0.803 for consolidation and 0.893 for effusion. Max pooling scored 0.677 and 0.804, and mean pooling 0.674 and 0.815.

Why it matters: it shows how to combine multi-zone POCUS exams that are incomplete or acquired remotely into one diagnosis, which is relevant to scoring tele-ultrasound sessions.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

TACROSS: An Efficient and Low-Cost Scalable Human Touch System Across Heterogeneous Tactile Sensors for Dexterous Robot Learning

Chen, Hu, Ma, …, Zhongliang Jiang, Liu, Chen · arXiv (cs.RO), 8 Oct 2026

A USD 10.86 piezoresistive glove with 285 sensing points records human touch. The paper aligns the glove and robot tactile sensors at the level of contact events, mapping both into a shared 256-dimensional latent. Robot demonstrations alone supervise the actions, while human glove data trains the tactile representation and provides confidence-weighted auxiliary guidance. Across four contact-rich tasks, the authors report 3.5× better efficiency than conventional teleoperation and a 95.7% lower equipment cost. They plan to release more than 150 h of tactile data.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Abdominal Ultrasound Simulation from Semantic Labels using Paired Label-to-Physics-Based Image Translation

Vitale, Deangeli, Larrabide, Orlando · arXiv (eess.IV), 2 Oct 2026

The pipeline has two stages. The first maps segmentations to a simplified image of the kind ray-casting produces, and the second refines it with a segmentation-guided CycleGAN. Pathologies and deformations are added by editing the label maps, so no patient CT is needed at inference. For stage I, a semantic diffusion model beat Pix2Pix on morphology (mIoU 0.43 vs 0.29), while Pix2Pix scored better on perceptual metrics (FID 0.32 vs 0.37).

Why it matters: it could supply cheap, varied training images for autonomous scanning and for simulators used to train tele-ultrasound operators.
