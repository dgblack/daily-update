Nigerian hospitals collaborate to perform tele-robotic surgery

Premium Times, 20 Sep 2026

Redeemer's Health Village, Nisa Premier Hospital (Abuja) and RoboMed Global report Nigeria's first tele-robotic surgery, a robot-assisted kidney removal for a tumour, run from a Toumai console at a different site.

Why it matters: another clinical telesurgery data point on the same long-distance, latency-sensitive teleoperation problem as your tele-ultrasound work.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Compliance for Free: Learning Identifiable Impedance via Bilateral Teleoperation

Guda, Colomé, Torras · arXiv 2609.19976, 17 Sep 2026

Uses the leader arm of a bilateral teleoperation setup as a separate measurement reference so stiffness becomes identifiable from existing joint-torque sensors, generating compliance labels for a vision-language-action model without annotation. Demonstrated on a wiping task ("firm" vs "normal" wipe).

Why it matters: bilateral teleoperation used as a data source for contact-rich, force-aware policies, relevant to learning from expert ultrasound scanning.

Affective Shared Autonomy: Temporal Affect Dynamics and Subjective Evaluation in Bimanual Teleoperation Tasks

Liang, Tian, Qu, Liu, Hu · arXiv 2609.19802, 17 Sep 2026

Monitors operator emotional state (face, heart rate, arm motion) and adjusts robot assistance, reporting a 39.7% increase in productive states without reducing user agency; releases a multimodal teleoperation dataset.
