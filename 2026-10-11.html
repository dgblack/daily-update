Comparing Haptic Cues for Operator Guidance Towards a Target Input with a One-Dimensional Active Lever

Conan, Farooq, Frissen, Cooperstock (McGill) · IEEE Trans Haptics, early access 8 Oct 2026 · doi:10.1109/TOH.2026.3736706

Twenty participants used a 1-DoF active lever to reach a target input, with different haptic cues guiding them. When visual guidance was also available, directional detents improved accuracy, while more salient cues made performance worse. Without visual guidance, directional vibrations beat proportional continuous force feedback and approached the acquisition times of visually guided trials.

Why it matters: this is direct evidence on which kind of haptic cue to use for guiding an operator to a target pose. That question applies to guiding a novice’s hand in human teleoperation, where strong force cues may hurt more than help.

A Multimodal Hyperspectral and Laser Speckle Imaging Platform for Simultaneous Monitoring of Spinal Cord Oxygenation, Perfusion and Metabolism

Junda Wang, Luca Giannoni, Ayse Gertrude Yenicelik, Eleni Giama, et al. · arXiv, 6 Oct 2026

An existing hyperspectral platform with 11 wavelengths (600–894 nm) gains laser speckle contrast imaging, so it can measure oxygenation and metabolism together with perfusion in the rat spinal cord. In phantom validation, the speckle signal had a strong positive linear relationship with pump-controlled flow.

Why it matters: combining hyperspectral and speckle imaging is a possible add-on for intraoperative hyperspectral systems in neurosurgery.

Beyond Report Imitation: Clinically Aware Multi-Image Ultrasound Report Generation from Visible Evidence

Yuchen Yang, Xin Wang, Lufan Wang, Yinghong Pan, et al. · arXiv, 8 Oct 2026

CAMEO writes ultrasound reports from several images and grounds each statement in findings that are actually visible, instead of imitating report templates. Compared with EchoVLM it raises BLEU-1 from 0.25 to 0.40, ROUGE-1 from 0.28 to 0.45, and ClinicalScore from 55.0 to 74.2.

Why it matters: automated reporting grounded in the images would be a useful last step after a remote or autonomous scan.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Hugging Suit: Pneumatically-Actuated System Design for Remote Haptic Experiences

Hespanhol, Hoggenmueller, et al. (University of Sydney; the author list in the arXiv metadata is malformed) · arXiv, 6 Oct 2026

A pneumatic wearable lets two people send and receive hugs remotely. Early user feedback suggests that dim, private settings combined with video increase emotional engagement. The paper reports no quantitative results.

RoboPrompt: Intuitive Robot Policy Steering with Sparse Human Input

Yanwen Zou, Chenyang Shi, Guoxuan Xu, Wenye Yu, et al. · arXiv, 7 Oct 2026

A pretrained policy is steered at run time by sparse human inputs, such as a drawn trace or a target point, without retraining. On three tasks, success with π0.5 rose by 15.5%, and human interventions fell by 44% and 82% in two settings. This is a lightweight alternative to full teleoperation for shared control.

Haptic Virtual Reality Training for Combined Spinal-Epidural Anesthesia in Anesthesiology Trainees: Prospective Randomized Controlled Pilot Trial

Xie, Lin, Zheng, Lin, …, Gao · JMIR Serious Games, 8 Oct 2026 · doi:10.2196/92207

In a randomized pilot with 20 interns, haptic VR training gave better overall clinical performance than manikin training, with a mean difference of 4.5 points (95% CI 1.85–7.15). Manikin training was better for aseptic technique.

Ultrasound

How well do routinely collected demographic and clinical variables aid point-of-care lung ultrasound TB classification

Jansen van Vüren, Geldenhuys, Parihar, Suttels, et al. · arXiv, 5 Oct 2026

Averaging the scores of a lung ultrasound image classifier and a separate clinical-variable classifier raised the AUROC for tuberculosis from 0.91 (image only) to 0.95. The extra point-of-care tests added little. This is relevant to low-resource screening with handheld probes.

Topology-Informed Prompt-Conditioned Universal Segmentation of Uterine Structures from Ultrasound and MRI

Yongheng Sun, Yuexi Gu, Jingwen Sun, Maureen Kohi, et al. · arXiv, 5 Oct 2026

A single graph-based, prompt-conditioned model segments uterine structures in both ultrasound and MRI. It reaches Dice scores of 0.898 and 0.693 on the two held-out test sets.

Computer vision & imaging

TAP3D: Thermal-Assisted 3D Human Point Clouds

Xie Zhang, Chengxiao Li, Xuan Liu, Chenshu Wu · arXiv, 8 Oct 2026

3D human point clouds are reconstructed from low-cost thermal arrays, with a 160K-sample dataset. Reported results are 4.87 cm mesh-recovery error, 21.86 cm tracking MAE and 91.5% fall-detection accuracy. This could offer a privacy-preserving way to sense body pose for patient modelling.
