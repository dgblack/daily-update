Robotic telesurgery in Europe: current status, clinical applications, and future perspectives in urology

Lavagno F, Spena G, Oderda M, … Andras I · Minim Invasive Ther Allied Technol, online 16 Nov 2026 · DOI

This narrative review covers European urological telesurgery from 2018 to 2026. Radical prostatectomy is the most common procedure, performed mainly on the Toumai and Edge platforms. The evidence is still isolated cases, small series and demonstrations at expert centres, and the authors say links should ideally be under 100 ms. They call for prospective data, harmonised regulation and standardised training, and suggest remote telementoring as an intermediate step.

Why it matters: it gives an up-to-date European overview and latency benchmark, timed alongside the FDA teleoperation workshop in early December.

Toward Resilient Self-Driving Laboratories through Telerobotic Interventions

Wu Z, Vecina Tercero A, Fatehi AA, … Deshpande N, Gongora AE · arXiv, 13 Nov 2026

Operators used a SpaceMouse to teleoperate a UR3e arm and recover from three kinds of lab-automation fault. The study compared a coupled control mode with a decoupled one. Decoupled control gave shorter completion times and less rotational motion. A second study found that operators without reference-path guidance were more target-driven and took more risks, and that guidance strategy changed how they behaved.

Why it matters: it is a small human-factors study of guidance and control coupling in human-in-the-loop teleoperation, which parallels the operator-guidance design choices in your mixed-reality teleoperation work.

Added Value of Shear Wave Elastography for Noninvasive Detection of Axillary Lymph Node Metastasis and IHC-Defined Surrogate Subtype Characterization in Breast Cancer

Chen J, Rosen DP, Adl Parvar T, … Alizad A · Cancers (Basel) 18(21):3549, 4 Nov 2026 · DOI

This prospective study included 141 women: 57 had reactive axillary nodes and 84 had metastatic nodes. Combining B-mode ultrasound with shear-wave elastography raised the AUC from 0.798 to 0.911, with 88.1% sensitivity and 82.5% specificity. Shear-wave speed statistics also differed between Luminal A and Luminal B subtypes, though the authors call this exploratory.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

An audiovisual assistance system for beam-stopper positioning in intraoperative electron radiotherapy: experimental evaluation under manual and teleoperated operation

Garrido-Rey L, Casans S, Deltoro JM, … Girbés-Juan V · Front Robot AI 13:1955069, 30 Oct 2026 (new in PubMed) · DOI

The system estimates in real time where the radiation beam meets the floor and gives audio and visual cues for placing the beam stopper. Guidance cut positioning error by about 80%, from roughly 0.30 m to under 0.07 m. This held under both manual and teleoperated control, with no increase in completion time and lower perceived workload.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

AI-Based Multi-View Echocardiographic Image Synthesis using Dynamic Shape Control

Freitas J, Balinisteanu A, Youssef A, Pereira VH, D'hooge J, Queirós S · arXiv, 13 Nov 2026 (accepted to IEEE IUS 2026)

The pipeline generates multi-view echo video from dynamic whole-heart meshes. An unpaired GAN-diffusion model first adds ultrasound appearance to each cross-section, and masked video reconstruction then makes the sequence temporally coherent. The abstract gives no quantitative results.

Physics-Based Differentiable Beamforming for Deep Learning-Based Contrast Enhanced Ultrasound Super-Resolution Imaging

Hameddine A, de Wilde B, Versluis M, et al. · arXiv, 6 Nov 2026

A network with a differentiable beamforming layer localises microbubbles directly from multi-channel RF data. The authors report sub-wavelength resolution in simulation.

Generative Active Learning for Label-Efficient Pan-Organ Ultrasound Diagnosis

Duan Y, Huang Y, Gao Y, et al. · arXiv, 6 Nov 2026

The method, EvoDiff, uses diffusion-guided search to choose which ultrasound images to label. The authors report near fully supervised performance across organs with far fewer labels.
