Remote robotic surgery for orthopedic trauma and spinal procedures: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Hammadeh BM, Al-Qunbar A, Hussein AM, et al. · J Robot Surg, epub 27 Nov 2026 · doi:10.1007/s11701-026-04242-3

The review pools 5 non-randomized studies from two Chinese centres (343 patients, 176 of them operated on remotely over 5G). All 176 remote procedures were completed as planned with no network failure. Operative time (SMD −0.12) and blood loss (SMD −0.23) did not differ significantly from local robot-assisted surgery, although the confidence intervals were wide. Pelvic and acetabular screw accuracy was 98.6% (213/216).

Why it matters: this is a pooled clinical safety baseline for long-distance telerobotics that you can cite when arguing that remote ultrasound is feasible.

A Deep Learning Based Robotic Ultrasound System for Automated Probe Alignment and Precise Finger Joint Scanning

Koo K, Ma G, Ragupathi S, et al. · IEEE T-MRB, epub 16 Nov 2026 · doi:10.1109/tmrb.2026.3753102

The system scans the whole hand under water, using an alignment network and compensation for underwater refraction. It found the scan centre to within 1–2 mm from initial errors of up to 1 cm. Experts graded 200 sweeps and gave a mean image-quality score of 3.33 ± 1.08 out of 5, with 77.5% rated good or better.

Quantitative Shear-Wave Elastography in Prostate Cancer: Lesion-Level Stiffness Thresholds and Association with Grade

Gurwin A, Patrzałek P, Nowak Ł, et al. · Cancers (Basel), epub 12 Nov 2026 · doi:10.3390/cancers18223615 · title shortened

The study is a retrospective analysis of 166 cancer foci in 132 men who had radical prostatectomy, matched to whole-mount pathology. Cancer was separated from benign tissue moderately well: AUC 0.765 for Emean (cut-off 54.5 kPa) and 0.797 for Emax (cut-off 75.5 kPa). Discrimination between grade groups was poor (AUC 0.595 and 0.583).

Why it matters: these are transrectal SWE reference thresholds to compare against micro-ultrasound elastography results.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

DataForgeVLA: Fully Synthetic Manipulation Demonstrations for Vision-Language-Action Training

Sergei Kurchev, Iaroslav Kolomiets, Filipp Maksimov, et al. (7 authors) · arXiv cs.RO, 25 Nov 2026

A fine-tuned video model generates demonstrations, and robot trajectories are recovered from the videos, so no teleoperation is needed. A policy trained on 300 accepted synthetic demonstrations succeeded in 16/20 real pick-and-place trials. A policy trained on 105 real teleoperated episodes succeeded in 18/20.

Multimodal image fusion system based on augmented reality and intraoperative ultrasound in laparoscopic anatomic hepatectomy

Li Y, Yang W, Wei Y, et al. · NPJ Precis Oncol, epub 14 Aug 2026 (newly indexed) · doi:10.1038/s41698-026-01652-2 · title shortened

The system fuses AR overlays with intraoperative ultrasound. In a retrospective cohort of 47 patients with hepatocellular carcinoma, using it was associated with shorter operations and inflow-occlusion times, less blood loss and better postoperative liver markers. The abstract gives no numbers for these comparisons.

Intraoperative Augmented Reality Visualization During Lymph Node Dissection in Cutaneous Malignancies

Israeli A, Yoschpe A, Shapira-Frommer R, et al. · Ann Surg Oncol, epub 26 Nov 2026 · doi:10.1245/s10434-026-20924-6 · title shortened

In a prospective pilot, a 3D model built from PET/CT was projected through an AR headset for 16 patients. Projection worked in all 16 cases with no technical failures or AR-related complications, and mean operative time was 106 min.

Augmented Reality Navigation Versus Patient-Specific Cutting Guides for Simulated Fronto-Orbital Surgery

Abazi S, Corten SFF, Beyer M, et al. · Bioengineering (Basel), epub 25 Oct 2026 · doi:10.3390/bioengineering13111258 · title shortened

The study compared optical see-through AR with CAD/CAM cutting guides on 20 printed skulls. Guides were more accurate (median deviation 0.54 mm vs 1.10 mm, adjusted p = 0.02) and faster (65.5 s vs 152 s per groove).

Inter-Operator Variability in Augmented Reality-Guided Breast Tumor Localization

Timóteo, Caleiro, Laborde, et al. · Authorea preprint, 27 Nov 2026 (seen as a Crossref record with abstract)

Thirteen surgeons marked tumours with AR under 8 visualization schemes for breast-conserving surgery. The mean error was about 12 mm, and differences between operators were the dominant factor.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

A Comprehensive 3D Ultrasound-Based Framework to Quantify Bulkamid® Deposition

Hadizadeh A, Badr D, Chill HH, et al. · Int Urogynecol J, epub 27 Nov 2026 · doi:10.1007/s00192-026-06974-0

Endovaginal 3D ultrasound was segmented in 31 women treated for stress urinary incontinence. A higher ratio of bulking-agent volume to urethral volume (0.16 vs 0.10, p = 0.03) was associated with clinical improvement in this exploratory analysis.

The Fetal Ear-Eye and Ear-Rotation Angle: A Pilot Study on Novel 3D Ultrasound Measurements

Musik TK, Monteiro S, Schönberger H, et al. · Fetal Diagn Ther, epub 15 Oct 2026 · doi:10.1159/fdt/acuag019 · title shortened

The study measured new 3D ultrasound angles in 102 fetuses, 20 of them with malformations. The ear-eye angle was higher in fetuses with malformations (median 3.29° vs 1.02°, p < 0.001), and agreement between observers was high (ICC 0.96).

Continuous Foundation-Guided Pseudo-Label Weighting for Breast Ultrasound Domain Adaptation

Xinyu Fan, Xinyi Li · arXiv cs.CV, 25 Nov 2026

The method weights pseudo-labels continuously for unsupervised domain adaptation across five breast ultrasound datasets. It gave the best mean Dice for targets adapted from BUS-BRA, but results elsewhere were mixed, and the authors call the work exploratory.

Computer vision & imaging

Hyperspectral Imaging Reveals Superior Preservation of Renal Microcirculatory Oxygenation with Crystalloid-Based Resuscitation

Rathenborg A, Özdemir B, Grützner P, et al. · Biomedicines, epub 9 Nov 2026 · doi:10.3390/biomedicines14112581 · title shortened

In a porcine model of haemorrhagic shock, hyperspectral imaging tracked renal tissue oxygen saturation (StO2), which fell from 73% to 59%. At 120 min, StO2 was 68% with crystalloid alone and 56% with crystalloid plus norepinephrine.

Indocyanine Green Fluorescence Angiography in Colorectal Cancer Surgery After a Decade of Randomized Trials

Son GM, Lee IY, Park S, et al. · Cancers (Basel), epub 18 Nov 2026 · doi:10.3390/cancers18223689 · title shortened

This narrative review covers ICG angiography, which cuts anastomotic leaks by about a third in meta-analyses, as well as hyperspectral and laser-speckle imaging. It argues that the open problem is quantifying perfusion, not visualizing it.
