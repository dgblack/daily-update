Top picks

Deep-Learning Enhanced Augmented Reality for Robotic Radical Prostatectomy on Consumer Grade Hardware

Benard V, Windisch O, Bjelogrlic M, … Valerio M · Surg Innov, online 6 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42990074

The AR pipeline masks surgical instruments so the overlay never covers them. The authors compared 21 encoders and chose FastViT-S12/U-Net, which reached a Dice of 95.1% on internal data and about 90–92% on two external sets. It runs at about 58 fps and 1.89 ms per inference on hardware costing about $1,150. On a da Vinci Xi, median photon-to-photon latency rose from 60 ms to 120.5 ms.

Why it matters: the paper actually measures what an MR overlay adds to end-to-end latency, the same budget that matters for overlays in tele-ultrasound and teleoperation.

ForceU-VLA: A Force-Aware Vision–Language–Action Model for Embodied Ultrasound Scanning and US-VLA: An Ultrasound Vision-Language-Action Model for Embodied Abdominal Scanning

Wu X, Zhang C, Yan G, Hu X, Liu Z, (Wu M), Cai Q · ACM Multimedia 2026 proceedings, dated 9 Nov 2026

The same group has two companion papers on VLA policies for autonomous robotic abdominal ultrasound, and one of them adds force awareness. I could only see the Crossref metadata: there is no abstract there, and the ACM page returned 403. So this is title-level information only.

Why it matters: VLA policies with force input are a direct competitor to, and possible source of autonomy for, human or model-mediated tele-ultrasound.

Trackerless Augmented Reality Navigation for Robotic Partial Nephrectomy using Simultaneous Localization and Mapping

Jagadeesan J, Huang E, Zhou H, … Chang SL · Research Square preprint, 27 Oct 2026 · PubMed 42990768

The system runs SLAM on stereo laparoscopic video, reconstructs the kidney surface, and registers it to the preoperative model without external trackers. This was a retrospective study of 20 cases on recorded video. Mean surface TRE was 1.47 mm, landmark error 4.3 mm, and localization error 2.6 mm. Nine surgeons rated the 2D overlay 4.41/5 and the 3D overlay 3.99/5.

Combining shear wave elastography and ultrasound localization microscopy for liver fibrosis staging in a rat model

Zhang Y, Lin H, Kong X, … Liu Z · Eur Radiol Exp 10:175, 6 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42993410

The study used 62 rats staged F0–F4 by histology. Combining viscoelastic SWE parameters with ULM microvascular features gave the best AUC for all four staging cut-offs (0.89–0.93). The work is preclinical only.

Why it matters: it fuses stiffness and microvasculature from one ultrasound platform, an approach that could carry over to multiparametric prostate micro-US and SWE.

Mixed reality guidance improves humeral head cut accuracy

Calem DB, Berisha L, Joshi T, … Erickson J · J Shoulder Elb Arthroplast 10(4):100105 · PubMed 42990785

Nine residents made 42 humeral head cuts on 3D-printed models. With passive HoloLens 2 holograms, neck-shaft angle deviation was 5.6°, against 10.4° both freehand and with an extramedullary guide, and cut-height deviation was 4.2 mm against 7.1–7.8 mm. Retroversion did not differ between groups.

Development of Anthropomorphic Arm for collaborative and Home Service Robot CHARMIE

Adil H, Ribeiro T, Ribeiro A, et al. · arXiv, 5 Nov 2026

An open-source, 3D-printed arm teleoperated through camera-based hand pose estimation. It is a companion to the CHARMIE VR teleoperation paper in yesterday’s report.

OUCH: Objective, Uniform, Controlled Haptic Stimulation for Automated Neurological Assessment

Choi S, Li SS, Romero J, et al. · arXiv, 3 Nov 2026

A pneumatic device worn on the wrist delivers repeatable noxious mechanical stimuli. In preliminary tests it caused discomfort at about 89% lower force than a handheld algometer. It points toward standardized physical exams that could be run remotely.

Ultrasound & elastography

Ultrasound vibro-elastography for quantitative assessment of ocular tissue biomechanics in patients with idiopathic intracranial hypertension: a pilot study

Bui NT, Kazemi A, Sit AJ, … Zhang X · Clin Biomech 141:107017, online 4 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42994193

The study compared 28 eyes from patients with 42 control eyes. Wave speed was higher in patients at the optic nerve head, optic nerve and sclera, by 1.57 m/s at the optic nerve head at 100 Hz. It correlated with papilledema grade (r = 0.66). The authors call the results preliminary.
