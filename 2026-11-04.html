A Mixed-Reality Virtual-Monitor Workspace for Endoscopic Pituitary Surgery: Proof of Concept from Bench to Bedside

Fontana C, Guacci M, Corrivetti F, Tortora A, Falchetta L, Meglio V, et al. · World Neurosurgery, online 3 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42977251

A HoloLens 2 shows the endoscope, neuronavigation and radiology feeds as voice-controlled virtual monitors. In a cadaver lab, 10 operators (5 consultants, 5 residents) gave it a SUS of 88 ± 6 and a SURG-TLX of 38 ± 17. The lead surgeon then wore it for an entire microadenoma resection without taking it off, with SUS 88 and SURG-TLX 52. The authors note that the study shows feasibility but did not test whether workload was reduced.

Why it matters: it is clinical evidence that a headset can be worn through a full operation, which supports MR as the operator display in your teleoperation interfaces.

Reducing operator-dependent variability in liver shear wave elastography through an AI-guided region-of-interest placement pipeline

Ali AR, Prakash S, Wang S, Kumar V, Guo P, Li Q, et al. · European Radiology, online 3 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42975708

The pipeline has two stages: SimCLR pretraining on 17,441 unlabeled images, then ENet fine-tuning. It automates liver segmentation, SWE ROI placement, probe guidance and skin-to-capsule distance. Across 18,182 images from 654 exams, segmentation reached Dice 0.89 and AUC 0.94. AI-to-attending agreement on ROI placement was 93.8%, against 81% between attendings, whose Cohen’s κ was only 0.25.

Why it matters: automated ROI and probe guidance is the kind of assistance a novice follower would need for remote or tele-guided elastography.

MABEL: An Open Anthropomorphic Mobile Bimanual Platform for Teleoperation and Learning

Cheng Q, Khorrambakht R, Kabouri MY, Nguyen QN, Macesanu L, Zhou H, Wang Y, Righetti L · arXiv (cs.RO), 2 Nov 2026

MABEL is an open-source mobile bimanual robot with 56 DoF and about $8.1k in parts. Apple Vision Pro hand and head tracking drives whole-body teleoperation without handheld controllers. Mean stereo video latency is 137 ms over the robot’s own Wi-Fi hotspot.

Why it matters: it is a cheap, open baseline for headset-driven teleoperation with a reported end-to-end video latency.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Pseudo-Haptics for Lowering Movements: Enhancing Perceived Weight via Initial Lifting Phase Modulation

Ban Y, Shimamura K, Ujitoko Y, Warisawa S · IEEE TVCG, 3 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42976056

Visual-delay pseudo-haptics conveyed weight less well when lowering an object than when lifting it (N=20), with large individual differences. Adding a visual delay only during the short initial lift before lowering strongly affected perceived heaviness (N=24, ηp² = 0.84).

Why it matters: it suggests a cheap way to convey force or weight in MR when real haptic feedback is not available.

Ultrasound & elastography

Perioperative shear wave elastography for the evaluation of spinal cord release in pediatric dysraphism: first experience in 21 patients

Gaume M, Richard L, Vergari C, Blondiaux E, Buffard M, Vialle R, de Saint-Denis T, et al. · arXiv (physics.med-ph), 3 Nov 2026; J Neurosurg Spine 45(4):536–542

SWE (Aixplorer) was performed under anesthesia before and after detethering in 21 children. Analyzable clips were obtained in 85% of patients. Mean cord stiffness fell from 25.2 to 11.2 kPa (p = 0.018), and there were no elastography-related complications. No pediatric normative values exist yet.

Computer vision & imaging

Development and validation of a reflectance-based approach for oxygen saturation estimation using high-speed hyperspectral imaging

Ngan C, Pruitt K, Fei B · Proc SPIE 13927 (2026), indexed 3 Nov · PubMed 42974614

StO₂ is estimated directly from reflectance instead of first converting to absorbance. Median error was 5.0% in blood phantoms and 5.3% in tissue-mimicking phantoms, versus 10.4% for the absorbance method, with blood-gas analyzer ground truth.

Hyperspectral imaging and machine learning in brain tumor surgery: Current evidence, clinical applications, and future directions

Kamran M · Surgical Neurology International 17:550, Oct 2026 · PubMed 42974249

This narrative review covers HSI+ML for tumor versus normal brain and for IDH status. It reports that spectral-spatial deep learning generally beats classical ML, and that adoption is held back by heterogeneous protocols, small cohorts and little multicenter validation. The abstract gives no new data.
