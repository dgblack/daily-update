Top picks

OpenH-RF: A Large-Scale Ultrasound Raw Channel Dataset Initiative

T.S.W. Stevens, S.W. Penninga, W.L. van Nierop, O. Nolan, F. Duelmer, B.N. Frey, et al. (186 authors) · arXiv, 9 Oct 2026

This is a community dataset of raw ultrasound channel data: about 40 TB from 19,332 acquisitions at 38 institutions, covering in-vivo, phantom and simulated data. The data use a single unified format that includes probe, acquisition and reconstruction metadata. Datasets are annotated for tasks including reconstruction, speed-of-sound estimation, aberration correction, flow, motion estimation, quantitative ultrasound and image interpretation. It is released under CC BY 4.0, and subsets can be streamed selectively.

Why it matters: this is a large open source of pre-beamformed data for beamforming, speed-of-sound and elastography methods, and for pretraining ultrasound foundation models.

MIRAGE: Multimodal Image-based Robotic Assistance and Guidance Environment for Surgical Navigation and Visualization

Ehsan Nasiri, Yi Zheng, Michael A. Kokko, Xiaoyao Fan, William R. Warner, Keith D. Paulsen, Ryan J. Halter · arXiv (cs.RO), 8 Oct 2026; under review at ASME J. Mechanisms and Robotics

MIRAGE is a single framework inside the da Vinci TilePro display. It combines stereo scene reconstruction, AR overlays, heads-up navigation and intraoperative ultrasound. It was validated on phantoms on the S, Si, Xi and SP systems. Mean 3D reprojection error was 0.6 mm across working distances, and the best result was 0.1 mm RMSE at 100 mm on the Xi. This is preclinical technical validation only.

Why it matters: it is close to your earlier work on intraoperative ultrasound and AR in the da Vinci console, and it offers a cross-platform baseline for comparison.

AI-Assisted Carotid Ultrasound for Nonexpert Use in Primary Care Prevention: User-Centered Design Study

Sjöström, Grönlund, Jonzén, …, Dahlin Almevall · J Med Internet Res, 8 Oct 2026 · doi:10.2196/105250

Fourteen nurse assistants at four primary care centres tested an AI-assisted carotid ultrasound prototype, and 15 were interviewed. The main usability problems were ergonomics, probe handling, navigating the interface, acquiring images, and time pressure. In response, the team added audio feedback, simplified data entry and revised the training.

Why it matters: this is qualitative evidence on what non-experts struggle with when they hold the probe. Remote expert guidance through human teleoperation targets those same barriers.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Remote Surfaces at Your Fingertips: Electrovibration-Based Tactile Feedback for Robot Teleoperation via Touchscreen Interfaces

Alperen Kenan, Juan José García Cárdenas, Adriana Tapus, Paul Bremner, Manuel Giuliani · arXiv, 22 Aug 2026 (older, but not reported before)

A touchscreen uses electrovibration to signal remote surface contact. This avoids the instability that kinesthetic feedback can show at rigid contact. In a study with 21 participants, tactile feedback reduced response time by 15.35% (p=0.002) and raised the sense of presence by 31% (p<0.001) compared with visual-only feedback. Workload and usability were similar in both conditions.

Mixed Reality Guidance in Robot-Assisted Laparoscopic Radical Nephrectomy

Zou, Cao, Xu, …, Zeng · J Robot Surg, 12 Oct 2026 · doi:10.1007/s11701-026-04048-3

This retrospective, non-randomized study compared 33 patients who had MR navigation with 41 who had conventional imaging. The MR group had shorter operative time (161 vs 182 min), less blood loss (median 150 vs 165 mL) and shorter hospital stays (median 6 vs 8 days). Complications and renal function did not differ. The authors call the results preliminary.

Global research landscape and hot-spot evolution of telesurgery in urology: a bibliometric analysis

Man, Shao, Yan, …, Yang · J Robot Surg, 12 Oct 2026 · doi:10.1007/s11701-026-03976-4

The analysis covers 181 Web of Science papers up to May 2026, with 35 published in 2025. The US leads in volume and collaboration, while China is growing fastest. Emerging topics are 5G networks, telepresence surgery and long-term follow-up. This is useful mainly for background and citations.

Ultrasound

Ultrasound and Magnetic Resonance Elastography of the Neck: Biomechanical Basis, Clinical Applications and Methodological Challenges

Rashedul Hasan, Anna Yang, Kishani Kandasamy, Hossein Bahreinizad, et al. · arXiv, 8 Oct 2026

This review covers neck tissue mechanics and elastography. It finds that ultrasound elastography is clinically useful for thyroid nodules and cervical lymph nodes, while neck MR elastography is feasible but still early. It proposes recommendations for standardised reporting.
