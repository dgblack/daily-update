Early applications of Apple Vision Pro in clinical medicine and medical education: a scoping review

Hu Y, Wang Y, Zuo X, …, Wang C · Frontiers in Medicine, 16 Oct 2026 · PubMed 42964997

The review maps early Vision Pro uses in diagnosis, surgical planning, intraoperative work, rehabilitation and teaching. Most of the evidence is feasibility studies, case reports or opinion pieces; the authors found only two Stage-2b confirmatory studies. They list the barriers as portability, cross-system compatibility, few medical apps, cost and certification.

Why it matters: it is a handy, current citation for related-work sections and grant text on MR headsets in medicine. It supports the claim that rigorous evaluations are scarce.

Shape-Change as a Haptic Modality: A Taxonomy and Survey of Hand-Contacting Shape-Changing Haptic Interfaces (SCHIs)

Yang Q, Zhou X, Hou J, Angelini L, Spiers AJ · IEEE Transactions on Haptics, online 29 Oct 2026 · PubMed 42955764

The paper proposes a three-axis “MIC” taxonomy of morphology, interaction mode and shape-information carrier. The carrier axis is new and was refined against 123 hand-contacting devices. The authors find that perception of these devices is still poorly characterized.

Why it matters: it gives a structured design space if you are considering non-force haptic cues, such as shape or pose guidance, for the human-teleoperation follower.
