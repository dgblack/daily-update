Safety and Usability of a Novel, Robotic Assistance System for Echocardiographic Examination

West C, Guppy-Coles K, Armstrong JW, Johnstone M, … Atherton JJ · Card Fail Rev, 2 Nov 2026 (indexed this week) · PubMed 43022928

In this single-arm trial, 30 adults (mean age 62.9) had a standard echo, with a sonographer driving the robot from the same room. There were no adverse events or device effects. Technical usability averaged 96.9%, 29 of 30 exams met diagnostic standards, and patient satisfaction averaged 8.3 out of 10.

Why it matters: this is clinical safety and usability evidence for robot-held echo, and the obvious next step is to run it remotely. It is a useful comparison point for human-teleoperation tele-echo.

ProtoCouncil: Multi-Encoder Anatomical Prototypes for Prostate Cancer Detection in Micro-Ultrasound

Abootorabi MM, Maghsoodi N, Saadat A, … Kinnaird A, Wodlinger B, Mousavi P, Abolmaesumi P · arXiv (cs.CV), 6 Nov 2026

Three foundation encoders (MedSAM, DINOv3 and V-JEPA 2.1) learn case-based prototypes at the level of the whole gland, the needle track and the gland sector. In patient-level cross-validation on 6,588 cores from 693 patients, the model reached 82.9% AUROC for clinically significant cancer (GG ≥ 3), 4.3 points above the best prior method. When the encoders disagree, the model abstains. On two external cohorts this raised balanced accuracy by 7 and 15 points while keeping 58% and 54% of cores.

Why it matters: this comes from the UBC/Queen's micro-US group with Exact Imaging (Wodlinger). It is directly relevant to your micro-ultrasound and elastography prostate work.

Clinical Significance of MRI-Suspicious Lesion Visibility on 29-MHz Micro-Ultrasound During MRI-Ultrasound Fusion Targeted Prostate Biopsy

Takayanagi A, Murayama S, Nishio Y, Saito Y, … Okabe K · Int J Urol 33(11), Nov 2026 · PubMed 43026638

This retrospective study included 238 patients. MRI-suspicious lesions were visible on micro-US in 71.8% of them. When the lesion was visible, GG ≥ 2 cancer was detected in 55.0% of patients, against 16.4% when it was not. Visibility stayed independently associated with GG ≥ 2 cancer (OR 3.80) and GG ≥ 3 cancer (OR 3.94).

Head-to-Head Comparison of Magic Leap 2 and HoloLens 2 Using Patient-Specific Phantoms in Orthopedic Oncology: A Feasibility Study

Nasir N, Cercenelli L, Stradiotti S, Fiore M, … Marcelli E · Sensors (Basel), 4 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43029764

Participants traced planned bone-tumour osteotomies on phantoms under AR guidance. With Magic Leap 2, 82%, 95% and 100% of traces fell within 1, 2 and 3 mm of the plan, against 65%, 85% and 95% with HoloLens 2. The authors present ML2's advantage only as a trend.

Why it matters: this is recent evidence on choosing between ML2 and HL2 for an MR guidance headset such as the one in your human-teleoperation follower setup.

Teleoperation, haptics & mixed reality

Reviewing haptics, sensory feedback & surgeon control in robotic urologic surgery

Sandberg M, Seoane J, Hemal A · Curr Opin Urol, 6 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43023922

This narrative review notes that urologic robots rely mainly on vision, sometimes supplemented with AR and auditory cues. It finds that feedback beyond vision and sound is still a gap, even though several studies show haptics improves surgeon performance. The abstract gives no figures.

HALO: Human-Aligned Data Collection for WhoLe-body MObile Manipulation

Yang S, Xu F, Shen Y, Zheng K, Xu Y · arXiv (cs.RO), 12 Nov 2026

Operators guide the arms kinesthetically and push a low-resistance base platform, with time alignment to the real base and inpainting to remove the demonstrator's hands from recorded images. It collects demonstrations up to 2.9× faster than Mobile ALOHA or VR teleoperation, and the resulting policies reach 73.3% success on a six-stage task. This is peripheral to your work: an alternative to teleoperation for collecting demonstrations.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Robotic ultrasound-guided biopsy, ablation, and access: a bibliometric analysis and validation-evidence map

Song X, Song Z, Li Z, Wu J, … Fu Z · J Robot Surg, 13 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43024139

Of 857 publications, 197 were about robotic systems and 70 of those reached human testing. Human-stage evaluation was common for biopsy (78.4%), less common for ablation (40.4%) and rare for vascular and other access (9.5%).

Why it matters: this is a ready-made citation for the claim that robotic ultrasound access and intervention rarely reach human testing.

Deep Learning-based Quantitative Ultrasound (DeepQUS) for Multiparametric Hepatic Steatosis Assessment

Lee A, El Kaffas A, Kamaya A, Dahl JJ, Baek J · arXiv (physics.med-ph), 8 Nov 2026

A U-Net combines H-scan, attenuation and Nakagami maps to estimate liver fat fraction pixel by pixel. Against MRI-PDFF in 30 patients, it reached a Spearman correlation of 0.92 and an AUC of 0.98.
