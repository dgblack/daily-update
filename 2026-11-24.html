Top picks

Exploiting Passive Low-Level Control Dissipating Capabilities to Leverage TDPA Passivity Layer in Teleoperation

Francesco Porcini, Camilla Celli, Valerio Novelli, et al. · IROS 2026, Crossref record 23 Nov 2026

From the title, the paper uses the energy that the robot's passive low-level controller already dissipates to relax the time-domain passivity approach (TDPA) layer. That would reduce how conservative the layer is. Crossref has no abstract, and I did not open the paper.

Why it matters: it bears directly on the stability–transparency trade-off in delayed bilateral tele-ultrasound.

Sensorless Four-Channel Control Architecture Using Inverse Dynamics Modeling for Human-Scale Bilateral Teleoperation

Amir Noohian, Dylan Miller, Justin Valentine, et al. · IROS 2026, Crossref record 23 Nov 2026

From the title, this is a four-channel bilateral architecture that estimates forces from an inverse dynamics model instead of using force sensors on human-scale arms. Crossref has no abstract.

Multiscan 3D ultrasound stitching for large-volume abdominal imaging: a phantom and prospective feasibility study in healthy volunteers and selected patients

Blank V, Schwabe P, Wiegand J, et al. · BMC Med Imaging, 23 Nov 2026 · DOI

The study involved 22 healthy volunteers. Stitched 3D ultrasound covered more organs than conventional ultrasound (27 vs 20) but had lower median image quality (3 vs 4), with both differences significant (Holm-adjusted P < 0.001). Across 44 participants, the stitched volumes allowed multiplanar review after the scan. The authors call the results exploratory and specific to their system.

Why it matters: this is a clinical baseline for large-volume 3D reconstruction, which is useful for store-and-review remote ultrasound.

Medtronic Hugo Robot Gains FDA Clearance for General Surgery

Unite.ai, 17 Nov 2026 (the page says the article is AI-generated and editor-reviewed)

The expanded 510(k) clearance reportedly covers upper-abdominal procedures (cholecystectomy, Roux-en-Y bypass, ventral hernia repair, small-bowel resection) as well as inguinal hernia repair and appendectomy. It follows Hugo's urology clearance in December 2025. I have not confirmed this against a Medtronic press release, so treat it as secondary.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

All of these are IROS 2026 papers with Crossref records from 23 Nov. Crossref has no abstracts for them, so each note is based on the title alone.

LATCH: Language-Affordance Teleoperation with Conflict-Aware Haptic Guidance

Dian Yuan, Xilong Sun, Zhencao Yang, et al.

Haptic shared control driven by language-grounded affordances, which detects when the guidance conflicts with the operator's intent. It continues the VLM shared-control theme from yesterday's SCG-VLM paper.

Safety-Critical Bilateral Teleoperation for Omnidirectional Aerial Manipulation Using Force-Sensorless Haptic Feedback

Yubin Kim, Jinwoo Lee, Yongjun You, et al.

Bilateral teleoperation of an aerial manipulator with safety constraints and haptic feedback that does not rely on force sensors.

Stiffness Copilot: An Impedance Policy for Contact-Rich Teleoperation

Yeping Wang, Zhengtong Xu, Pornthep Preechayasomboon, et al.

A policy that sets the robot's impedance during contact-rich teleoperation. It could apply to regulating probe contact.

Force Feedback for Robust Excavator Teleoperation under Visual Uncertainty

Solmon Jeong, Eran Beeri Bamani, Joao Buzzatto, et al.

Force feedback used to make up for degraded visual information during remote operation of an excavator.

ViHaTeleop: A Low-Cost, Lightweight Visual-Haptic Teleoperation System for Dexterous Manipulation Learning

Fucai Zhu, Yanhou Lai, Paul Maestre, et al.

A low-cost visual and haptic teleoperation rig for collecting demonstrations of dexterous manipulation.

Self-Supervised Monitoring of Command–Behavior Consistency on a Teleoperated Robot via Action-Conditioned Prediction

Eduardo Fraga Da Silva, Fendy Santoso, Willy Susilo, et al.

A learned predictor flags when the teleoperated robot's behaviour stops matching the commands it receives. This may be relevant to fault and attack detection in remote procedures.

Remote health & medical robotics

Current cybersecurity threats to patient safety in the operating room: a review

Ahmed MM, Kasimieh O, Adebayo UO, et al. · Patient Saf Surg, 2026 (newly indexed in PubMed) · DOI

This narrative review covers threats to Internet of Medical Things devices in surgery, including attacks on teleoperated robots, ransomware and supply-chain compromise. It notes that most of the evidence comes from healthcare in general rather than from surgery, and the abstract gives no pooled numbers.

Accuracy and Efficiency of Proprietary Navigation, Haptic, and Optical Robotic Systems: An In Vitro Study

Li Y, Wang W, Shen Y, et al. · Clin Implant Dent Relat Res, Dec 2026 · DOI

In this in vitro study, 480 dental implants were placed in 120 models. Angular deviation was 0.95° with the haptic robot and 0.85° with the optical robot, compared with 1.37° for dynamic navigation (p < 0.001). The haptic robot was fastest, at 221 s per implant against 313 s for navigation.
