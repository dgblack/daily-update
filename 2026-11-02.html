Machine learning-assisted intraoperative hyperspectral imaging in cranial meningioma surgery: interim results of an ongoing study

Pfahl A, Weber F, Vychopen M, Arlt F, Melzer A, Barrantes-Freer A, Güresir E, Köhler H, Wach J · Neurosurgical Focus 61(5):E10, 1 Nov 2026 · PubMed 42967340

This is a single-centre prospective study (Leipzig), with 70 patients analysed so far. Expert-annotated hyperspectral regions of meningioma, cortex and dura were classified using leave-one-patient-out validation. An MLP on SNV-normalised spectra separated meningioma from cortex with 0.93 accuracy and MCC 0.84. The three-class task (adding dura) was harder, at 0.77 accuracy. Meningioma showed lower oxyhemoglobin features and lower tissue oxygenation.

Why it matters: it is a recent clinical HSI benchmark next to your fluorescence/HSI glioma work. Note the pixel-level vs patient-level caveat the authors themselves raise.

Investigating Points of View and Their User Control in Collaborative Heterogeneous Mixed Reality

Fevre T, Chaffangeon Caillet A, Fleury C, Nigay L · IEEE TVCG, online 29 Oct 2026 · PubMed 42955742

The authors build a design space for coupled viewpoints in remote-expert/on-site-operator MR systems and use it to classify 28 existing systems. In most of them, the operator controls the shared view. A study with 18 pairs compared operator, expert and dual “mixed” control. Expert-only control was worst. Mixed control matched operator control on performance, was preferred, and was rated less demanding by experts.

Why it matters: it bears directly on who drives the remote expert’s viewpoint in human-teleoperation tele-US. It is also a useful taxonomy to cite.

Sensorium: An Open-Source Multimodal Data Collection and Curation Library for Embodied Surgical AI

Mazza L, Rodriguez A, Speidel S · arXiv, 30 Oct 2026 (NeurIPS 2026 GenAI for Health workshop)

Sensorium is a Python library for ROS/ROS2 that records, processes, annotates and packages multimodal robot and sensor streams into episodic datasets. It was evaluated on 1,516 phantom, ex vivo and in vivo porcine recordings, with preliminary usability feedback.

Why it matters: it could serve as off-the-shelf plumbing for logging synchronized force, pose and US streams from teleoperated scans for later policy learning.

A broad review of literature from 1998 to 2024 on networked haptics in telemedicine, VR/AR and remote collaboration. It names cost, latency, interoperability and bandwidth as the barriers. It is lightweight, so only cite it as background if needed.
