Transcontinental robot-assisted telesurgery in the 21st century: global progress, clinical realities, and implications for urology, a narrative review

Ahallal Y, Frey S, Assaid N, Chand M, Mottrie A · Canadian Journal of Urology, online 18 Nov 2026 · DOI

The authors reviewed human transcontinental cases from 2000 to 2025 and identified seven landmark procedures on the ZEUS, Edge and Toumai systems, at distances of up to 11,000 km. All were completed without conversion or major intraoperative complications, with latency kept under 200 ms. They conclude that safety depends on robust networks and an experienced on-site team, and that regulatory harmonisation and more clinical validation are still needed.

Why it matters: it is a compact, citable summary of clinical long-distance telesurgery and its latency envelope, useful for framing tele-ultrasound proposals.

Digital-Twin-Enabled Synchronous Teleoperation of Robotic Arms Using BiLSTM-KF Hybrid Model for Time Delay Compensation

Sun M, Liu Y, Liu Z, Huang P · Symmetry, 17 Nov 2026

A BiLSTM predicts the communication delay one step ahead and a Kalman filter corrects the estimate recursively. The corrected delay drives a feedforward speed adjustment of the local virtual arm, so the remote arm's low-level controller is left unchanged. Delay prediction reached an RMSE of 5.6 ms. Normalised position deviation between the virtual and remote arms was 2.4 ± 0.6%, which is 54% lower than with no compensation and 36% lower than an LSTM-only baseline.

Why it matters: learning-based delay prediction is a simple add-on for keeping a local virtual model in sync, as in model-mediated teleoperation.

Hidden Variables Behind Real-Robot Data Quality: Lessons from Hundreds of Thousands of Hours

Maniformer Real-World Manipulation Team, … Wang W · arXiv cs.RO, 17 Nov 2026

Drawing on a very large demonstration corpus, the team identifies workspace limits, control-interface friction and latency as causes of pauses, detours and corrective motions, even in demonstrations that succeed. Running control of different body parts in parallel cut task completion time by about 43–53%. Compliant control improved success and lowered peak contact forces on contact-rich tasks. The abstract does not quantify the effect of latency itself.

Why it matters: it gives large-scale evidence that teleoperation interface design shapes operator behaviour, which applies to human-teleoperation interfaces too.

Teleoperation & haptics

Haptic Probing for Subsurface-Aware Force Distribution on Layered Soft Tissue

Shi L, Wang X, … Li X · arXiv cs.RO, 17 Nov 2026

Under a light preload, a brief low-amplitude excitation classifies the tissue as muscle or bone-backed from its dynamic admittance relative to a per-subject baseline. This happens before the task force is applied. A distribution law then equalises fingertip pressure and sets the allowable pressure for that tissue type. A passivity budget covers probing, mode switching and contact recruitment. The method was tested on three human subjects, and the abstract gives no numbers.

Why it matters: admittance-based tissue probing and passivity budgeting carry over to force-controlled probe contact in robotic ultrasound.

MoViC: Real-Time Humanoid Whole-Body Control from Monocular Video Streams

Han Y, Zhang J, … Lu P · arXiv cs.RO, 15 Nov 2026

The pipeline combines monocular human motion reconstruction, retargeting and a transformer whole-body policy, and lets an operator teleoperate a humanoid from a live RGB camera with no wearables. An extrapolation step bridges the roughly 10 Hz pose estimates to a 50 Hz controller. No end-to-end latency figures are given on the abstract page.

Ultrasound & imaging

DAP-MR: Degradation-Aware Prompt-Magnitude Routing for Real-World Ultrasound Super-Resolution

Le N-H-L, … Huang C-C · arXiv cs.CV, 17 Nov 2026

Diffusion super-resolution is conditioned on learned degradation tokens rather than semantic text prompts. A router predicts how strongly each degradation should be corrected for a given input. The method plugs into one-step and multi-step diffusion SR, and the authors report better quality and cross-domain generalisation, including on unpaired real ultrasound. No numbers appear in the abstract.

Why it matters: it may help enhance low-end or handheld probe images, or compressed tele-ultrasound streams.
