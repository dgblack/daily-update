UltraVoxelGS: Voxel-First Feed-Forward Gaussian Splatting for 3D Ultrasound Reconstruction

Yue Wang, Haoxin Chen, Xiaoqing Guo · arXiv, 9 Nov 2026

The network predicts Gaussians directly from posed 2D slices, so there is no per-scene optimization. A voxel-to-Gaussian pass keeps the representation a fixed size, and the renderer separates reflection from cumulative attenuation. Reconstruction takes about 10 s instead of 10–20 minutes (more than 35× faster) and scales to sequences of over 1,000 frames. The authors report the best PSNR and SSIM against per-scene baselines on real and simulated data.

Why it matters: reconstruction this fast is close to live, which could give the remote expert in tele-ultrasound 3D spatial feedback while the scan is still going.

Compact and Wide-Force-Range Magnetorheological Hand Exoskeleton for Kinesthetic Feedback in Teleoperation

Mai X, Yu R, Chen R, Leonardis D, Li W, Chiaradia D, Frisoli A, Sun S · arXiv, 9 Nov 2026

MR brakes give 0.01–0.918 N·m of braking torque and up to 21 N at the fingertip, with a total mass of 39.8 g and 1.01 N of back-drive force. Tele-grasping succeeded every time with or without the feedback, but kinesthetic feedback cut contact force by 28.2% compared with vision alone. Users could also tell object stiffness apart using the feedback alone.

Simulating Vein Compressibility for Ultrasound-Guided Femoral Access: Speed, Accuracy, and Phantom Validation

Manicka A, Coulon L, Ang D, Loushin M, Kowalewski TM · arXiv, 9 Nov 2026

The authors model probe-induced deformation of the femoral vessels in Isaac Sim for Healthcare and check it against a benchtop phantom. The artery stayed within 5 percentage points at every depth, and the vein within 3 points through 11 mm of a 25.4 mm press. A tuned solver setting cut solver time by 976×, to 30.4 ms per frame, or 56.4 ms with US and graphics rendering. With literature tissue properties the model reproduces the clinical sign that the vein collapses before the artery.

Why it matters: a fast, validated deformable patient model like this could serve as the environment model in model-mediated tele-ultrasound, or as a place to train autonomous scanning policies.

Scaling Telesurgery—Safety, Equity, and Governance

Violante T, Sassun R, Rottoli M, Perry WR · JAMA Surgery Viewpoint, online 11 Nov 2026

This viewpoint looks at where telesurgery is technically feasible and what still blocks routine use. It proposes standards for evidence, accountability, training and local capacity. I saw only the Crossref metadata and abstract, not the full text.

Augmented reality for robotic surgery: a feasibility study and comparison of registration algorithms (robot-assisted esophagectomy)

Kuiper GM, van der Duin J, de Jong RLPD, … Pluim JPW, Ruurda JP · Surg Endosc, online 10 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43007453

The study included 10 consecutive patients. It compared one manual and six automated methods for registering CT-based 3D models to stereo-derived intraoperative point clouds. Manual registration had the highest success rate (0.80, mean surface distance 11.5 mm), while the automated methods succeeded in only 0.10–0.40 of cases, though each ran in under 3 s. Surface distance could not separate acceptable from rejected registrations. The authors say deformable registration is needed before AR guidance can be relied on.

Teleoperation, haptics & mixed reality

A wearable input/output interface learns personalized models of haptic perception

Li W, Adams EM, Xu Z, … Ni X, Tian B, Lopes P · arXiv, 6 Nov 2026

The device pairs a 36-channel electrotactile array with capacitive touch sensing, so stimulation sites and the locations users report share one body frame. Across 24 participants the electrotactile and referred-thermal maps varied a lot between people. A human-in-the-loop calibration then learns each user's map, and the authors show it working in haptic-guided robot teleoperation and in VR.

Exploring performance and trust in autonomous surgical robotic systems: the role of visuo-haptic guidance

Papenmeier F, Hagmann K, Blossey L, … Huff M, Klodmann J · IJCARS, online 7 Nov 2026

In this study, 34 participants took over a ring-transfer task from an autonomous robot. With visuo-haptic guidance they detected failures faster than with visual guidance alone, but completion time and accuracy did not improve. The haptic guidance raised trust and perceived control, along with physical demand and effort. (Last report listed this under Other Links; the abstract is now on PubMed.)

A human-in-the-loop Control Paradigm for Construction Robotics: From VR-Guided Teleoperation to Semi-Autonomous Dual-Arm Operation

Chen J, Xu X, Liao Z, et al. · arXiv, 10 Nov 2026

For dual-arm construction tasks, the framework switches between VR teleoperation, shared autonomy and full autonomy. The semi-autonomous mode coped better with disturbances and reduced operator workload.

Does Haptic Connection to a Healthy Partner Improve Motor Learning in Chronic Stroke? A Pilot Study of Robot-Based Dyadic Training

Waters EL, Stroutinsky-Sobotka N, Azar RM, et al. · arXiv, 9 Nov 2026

Stroke patients haptically coupled to a healthy partner performed better on a tracking task and showed more effort and movement complexity. Neither the coupled nor the solo group showed clear retention.

Prioritizing Human Factors in the Clinical Translation of Mixed Reality Surgical Guidance

Bahadori S, Buckle P · JMIR Hum Factors 13:e101995, 9 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43002763

This viewpoint argues that MR guidance should be judged on usability, physical tolerability and robustness in the operating room as well as on accuracy. It names three human-factors domains: physical characteristics, dexterity and motor demands, and the environment (noise, glare, occlusion). It also calls for systems-level attention to teams, training and governance.

Novel Use of Augmented Reality-Guided Ventriculoperitoneal Shunt Proximal Catheter Placement Using the VisAR system

Charcos I, Ifrach J, Thomas AJ, Mossop CM · Neurosurg Pract 7(6), 9 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43006045

In this single case report, the proximal catheter went in on the first pass under headset AR guidance, and postoperative CT matched the plan.

Ultrasound & elastography

FAST Performance Changes After a Training Sequence Including Augmented Reality Guidance Among Paramedic Students

Hama K, Suga R, Katsuki A, … Yokota H · Simul Healthc, online 9 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43007262

Eighteen paramedic students did conventional and then AR-guided FAST exams after two hours of training. Median total exam time fell by 264 s, and checklist and global rating scores improved (both P<0.001). The order was fixed and there was no control group, so the gain cannot be put down to AR alone.

Why it matters: it is evidence that AR guidance helps novice ultrasound operators, which is the premise behind human teleoperation for remote ultrasound.

Combined quantitative speed-of-sound imaging and shear-wave elastography for differentiating benign and malignant breast lesions

Liao C, Zhao L, Huang D, … Zhang B · Quant Imaging Med Surg 16(11):880 · PubMed 42997928

This was a retrospective study of 108 lesions. A nomogram combining B-mode features, SWE maximum elasticity (70.6 vs 40.9 kPa) and speed of sound reached an AUC of 0.969, with 88.4% sensitivity and 92.3% specificity. It was validated internally only.

Remote health & communications

5G-NetBotSim: A Synchronized Co-Simulation Framework for Connected Robots over ROS and srsRAN-based 5G Network

Golmohammadi N, Garuda BVR, Joshi A, Baidya S · arXiv (cs.NI), 6 Nov 2026

The framework connects ROS/Gazebo to a full-stack srsRAN 5G emulator in real time and logs timing at every stage. Changes in CQI and MCS carry through to latency and to teleoperation and leader–follower performance. Better channel quality cut tracking error from 0.558 m to 0.460 m.

Why it matters: it offers a realistic, repeatable way to test teleoperation over 5G, in place of the usual fixed artificial delays.

VISTA: A Benchmark for Real-Time Video Streaming under Network Impairments in Surgical Teleoperation and K-AoI: A Spatio-Temporal Synchronization Metric for Digital Twin-based Teleoperation in Dynamic Network Environments

Deng Z, Yuan Z, Tian L, et al. (Warwick) · IEEE Smart World Congress 2026, indexed 9 Nov

These are two papers from the Warwick group, which also wrote LiteSC on semantic telesurgical video. One is a benchmark for streaming surgical video over impaired networks; the other is an age-of-information metric for keeping a digital twin in sync during teleoperation. Industry, news & social

GE HealthCare adds Lung Guidance and AutoAS to Vscan Air SL

GE HealthCare press release · 3 Nov 2026

Two FDA 510(k)-cleared, CE-marked AI features are coming to the handheld Vscan Air SL. Lung Guidance gives real-time probe guidance through the 8-zone lung protocol, and in validation non-experts acquired diagnostic exams 95.2% of the time against 96.8% for experts. AutoAS flags likely moderate-to-severe aortic stenosis from two 2D views. Subscribers should get both by the end of November.

Why it matters: AI probe guidance for novices is a commercial alternative to expert tele-guidance, and it competes with the same use case.
