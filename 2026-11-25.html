TeleMultiStream: An Integrated Robot Teleoperation System with Low-Latency Multi-Stream Video Feedback

Chenchen Lv, Lingchen Hu, Yaosai Zhang, et al. · arXiv cs.RO, 23 Nov 2026

The system sends several camera streams over WebRTC and robot poses over UDP. On a wired LAN with a ZED Mini, the median glass-to-glass latency was 60 ms with software encoding and 66 ms with hardware encoding, against an 82 ms reference. Mean one-way UDP latency at 100 Hz was about 1.6–1.8 ms, with estimated packet loss of 0.0033%.

Why it matters: it gives a concrete baseline for the video part of the latency budget in mixed-reality tele-ultrasound. The figures are LAN-only, so they do not show WAN or 5G performance.

XFreq: Geometry-Aligned Cross-View Residual Learning for Sparse 3D Ultrasound Reconstruction

Yuhuan Lu, P. Ellen Grant, Davood Karimi · arXiv cs.CV, 23 Nov 2026

XFreq builds 3D ultrasound volumes from a few tracked 2D slices whose poses are known. It uses separate low- and high-frequency decoders joined by a gating module. It was evaluated on an in-house fetal brain dataset and the public CETUS cardiac dataset, and it reached 26.82 dB PSNR on CETUS with 32 input slices.

Why it matters: the inputs are pose-tracked sparse sweeps, which is what a teleoperated or freehand remote scan produces.

An XR-Based System for Enhanced Spatial Awareness in Mobile Robot Teleoperation

Subhransu Sourav Priyadarshan, Valentina Pericu, Federico Rollo, Gianni Viardo Vercelli, Navvab Kashiri · arXiv cs.RO, 24 Nov 2026 (IEEE/ASME AIM 2026)

An XR interface shows forward and rear camera views. A 16-channel LiDAR drives vibrotactile feedback on the hand controllers to signal nearby obstacles. In a study with 20 participants, the haptic cues significantly reduced the cognitive load of avoiding obstacles. The abstract gives no other numbers.

Feasibility of Multifrequency Neural Network-Based Wave Inversion in Ultrasound Time-Harmonic Elastography

Héloïse Bustin, Tom Meyer, Paul Spiesecke, Jakob Jordan, Anja Hennemuth, Ingolf Sack · arXiv eess.IV, 20 Nov 2026

The authors move the MF-ElastoNet wave-inversion method from MR elastography to ultrasound time-harmonic elastography. They tested it on phantoms and on liver scans from 4 healthy volunteers and 14 patients. Compared with k-MDEV, the mean bias was −0.02 m/s, with 95% limits of agreement from −0.36 to 0.33 m/s.

Why it matters: a learned multifrequency inversion could also be used for external-excitation elastography of the prostate.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Self-Sensing Compliant Anthropomorphic Hand for Speed-Dictated Shared Force Control in Teleoperation

Huijiang Wang, Yu Zhang, Rui Chen, et al. (Josie Hughes group) · arXiv cs.RO, 24 Nov 2026

Potentiometers read the hand's elastic tendon transmission, which turns it into a sensor for joint states and finger forces. A contact-aware shared-control layer for VR teleoperation uses those estimates. The authors report higher grasp success than direct teleoperation with fragile and heavy objects, but the abstract gives no figures.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

US-Seg: One Reference for Universal Ultrasound Segmentation

Chen Ma, Yunshu Li, Shuyu Liang, et al. · arXiv eess.IV, 21 Nov 2026

US-Seg segments a target in an ultrasound image from a single annotated reference image, with no finetuning. Across six cross-site and cross-task tests it averaged 81.85% Dice. The authors say that is 24.30% above the strongest label-reference baseline.
