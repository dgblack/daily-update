Top picks

A Collaborative Path Planning Strategy for Robotic Soft-Tissue Surgery

Caputo CC, Krause AC, Meza-Pantoja A, et al. · J Med Robot Res 11(1–2):2650005 (epub Mar 2026, newly indexed in PubMed) · doi:10.1142/s2424905x26500054

The surgeon picks key points on the tissue with a haptic device and a multi-camera 3D overlay, and an autonomous planner fills in the rest of the path. The authors present this as a middle ground between teleoperation and full autonomy. In a human-subject study it cut waypoint spacing error by 67.6%, path-correction probability by 33.3%, completion time by 40.7% and perceived workload by 30.5%, compared with unassisted path generation.

Why it matters: it measures shared autonomy layered on haptic teleoperation, the same split you would need when remote ultrasound or surgery hands some tasks to local autonomy.

RoboJackVR: Turning Robot Tasks into VR Games for Scalable Demonstration Collection

Ben Ma, Jixuan Cai · arXiv cs.RO/cs.HC, 25 Nov 2026

RoboJackVR is a standalone VR game in which players control simulated robots, with MuJoCo running on the headset, and their play is recorded as demonstrations. ACT policies trained only on these VR demonstrations kept about 91% of the success rate of a baseline trained on standard simulation demonstrations, across 66 LIBERO-90 tasks and four seeds. The robots are simulated only; there is no physical teleoperation.

EgoLoAF: Learning Humanoid Loco-Manipulation from Egocentric Demonstrations with Hand-Object Affordances

Yiming Zeng, Jiangyuxuan Dai, Yuheng Na, et al. (15 authors) · arXiv cs.RO, 24 Nov 2026

The authors turn egocentric human video into humanoid training data without wearable motion trackers, and release 128,400 episodes (1,000 hours, 1,284 tasks). On four real-world tasks the method scored 73.1, close to the 74.4 scored with teleoperated data (mean task-progress score), while collecting data 4.03× faster.

Why it matters: it gives a direct comparison of teleoperated and egocentric data quality, which is useful context if you plan to learn from tele-ultrasound sessions.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Mixed reality-guided navigation system for standard temporomandibular joint prosthesis placement: a preclinical validation study

Zhang X, Fan X, Zhao J, et al. · Int J Oral Maxillofac Surg, epub 25 Nov 2026 · doi:10.1016/j.ijom.2026.11.014

The system combines a HoloLens with optical navigation and was tested on 10 skull models. Maximal fossa-prosthesis deviation was 1.14 ± 0.69 mm with mixed reality and 0.84 ± 0.21 mm with digital templates (P = 0.213). Ramus-angle deviation was 2.07° versus 2.58° (P = 0.524). The study was in vitro only.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Structured Noise Diffusion for Echocardiography Haze Removal

Zike Luo, Hao Li, Mengxiao Wang, et al. · arXiv cs.CV, 25 Nov 2026

The diffusion model treats echo haze as localized Gaussian components constrained by the fan geometry. It achieved the highest mean generalized contrast-to-noise ratio on the public DehazingEcho2025 benchmark (0.715 ± 0.090) and on two private cohorts.

TAT-Det: A Dataset for Testicular Appendage Torsion Detection and Segmentation in Ultrasound Images

Ying Jiang, Juntao Jiang, Linxuan Fan, et al. · arXiv cs.CV, 25 Nov 2026

The dataset has 2,600 expert-annotated images from 1,203 patients at the Children's Hospital, Zhejiang University, with bounding boxes and masks. Among the baselines, YOLO11m was best at detection and RWKV-UNet was best at segmentation.

Computer vision & imaging

HuluOPD: Unifying Heterogeneous Medical Specialists into One Lightweight Model with Unlabeled Prompts

Yuncheng Zhang, Songtao Jiang, Yitian Tang, et al. · arXiv cs.CV, 24 Nov 2026

Five medical vision-language specialists (ultrasound, pathology, OCT, fundus, dental) are distilled into one model using 914k unlabeled prompts. A model with 10% of the specialists' combined parameters keeps 94% of their QA performance on 132k test items, and one with 2% keeps 89%.

Philips introduces Skylight Device Manager to turn ultrasound data into operational intelligence

Royal Philips press release (via GlobeNewswire/Finviz) · 18 Nov 2026

Skylight is a cloud application that gives one view of usage, operations and service across a fleet of connected ultrasound systems. It is offered through the OneSpace platform and is available only in the US for now. Diagnostic X-ray is planned next, and the app will be shown at RSNA 2026.
