Top picks

ARTS: An Open-Source, Reproducible Three-Arm Platform for Autonomy Research in Minimally Invasive Surgery

Ge J, Kim C, Goldenberg A, … Unberath M, Cleary K, Krieger A · arXiv (cs.RO), 11 Nov 2026

ARTS is a three-arm teleoperated laparoscopic platform (UR5e-class arms) built for about US$137k from parts you can buy. The console has haptic handles, footswitches and a stereo viewer, plus a hardware stop that freezes all arms. Hand-to-arm latency is 58 ms and endoscope-to-monitor latency is 68 ms. ISO 9283 repeatability is 0.14–0.25 mm, which the authors say is comparable to a dVRK Classic arm, and the remote-centre-of-motion error is 0.34–0.37 mm. The test tasks were FLS peg transfer (three experts and three novices) and suturing (one expert).

Why it matters: this is an open alternative to the dVRK built on industrial arms, and its latency budget is a useful reference for teleoperation research.

I See What You Want: Decoding Hierarchical Operator Intent from Visual Teleoperation History

Jia X, Qu K, Wang S, Yang C, Katzschmann R, Hutter M · arXiv (cs.RO), 12 Nov 2026

VIAMO is a dataset of 6,600 episodes from 11 robot sources, labelled with both atomic actions and missions. A fine-tuned VLM (VIM) predicts the operator's current action and mission from a short history of teleoperation video. Its Sentence-BERT similarity is 0.825 for actions and 0.821 for missions, against 0.563 and 0.470 for GPT-4o. In a takeover test, an autonomous policy took over mid-task using VIM's predicted mission, cutting human teleoperation time by 34.6% compared with using GPT-4o.

Why it matters: inferring intent is the core of shared autonomy. In tele-ultrasound, the same idea could let the robot or an AI finish a scan the expert has started.

RUMI: A Robotiq-Matched Universal Manipulation Interface for Robot Learning with Augmented Reality and Haptic Feedback

Avdiu K, Bien S, Blei Y, … Burgard W · arXiv (cs.RO), 11 Nov 2026

RUMI is a handheld teleoperation and demonstration device whose geometry follows the nonlinear mechanics of the Robotiq 2F-85 gripper. It has exchangeable fingertip force sensors and a haptic mechanism for force-aware teleoperation. A VR-headset AR overlay shows whether a motion is kinematically feasible for the robot. With the overlay, joint-limit violations fell from 16 of 50 episodes to 3 of 50. The abstract gives no numbers for the haptic feedback.

Why it matters: showing feasibility in AR is a cheap way to keep a human operator inside the robot's workspace, which is the same problem as guiding a human follower in human teleoperation.

Integrating extended reality into preoperative neurosurgical planning: exploratory evaluation

Drechslerová A, Widhalm G, Hangel G, Rajabi N, et al. · Front Digit Health, 28 Oct 2026 (indexed this week) · PubMed 43017536

The authors linked a 3D Slicer desktop to an XR workspace for patient-specific planning. Twenty participants with mixed experience each planned one glioblastoma case and one low-grade glioma case, and every session finished without a session-ending technical interruption. Directional convergence of the entry-to-tumour trajectories was 0.881 for the GBM and 0.965 for the LGG. Agreement on which anatomical structures to select was only fair (κ = 0.397). The authors say these measures are descriptive only.

Why it matters: this is the Vienna glioma group (Widhalm), which also works on 5-ALA fluorescence. It is close to your hyperspectral and fluorescence-guided brain tumour work.

Ultrasound & elastography

Ultrasound Elastography: Excitation Methods and Low-Cost Alternative

Otesteanu CF · arXiv, 11 Nov 2026 (journal ref: IWSSIP 2019)

A smartphone serves as the mechanical exciter, and the elasticity inverse problem is solved with FEM. Preliminary phantom reconstructions look similar to those from a dedicated harmonic actuator, but the abstract gives no numbers. This is an older conference paper that has only now been posted.

Why it matters: cheap external excitation is relevant to low-cost or remote vibro-elastography setups.

Smart Spectral Doppler Ultrasound Imaging using Motion Tracking based on Wide-beam Transmission and Neural Networks: Preliminary Results

Baek J, Lee A, Kang J, et al. · arXiv, 6 Nov 2026

The method uses a single wide-beam transmit and a neural network to track vessel motion, so the Doppler sample volume stays on target. In vivo, spectral SNR rose by 9.3 dB in the carotid and 4.5 dB in the hepatic vein.

Why it matters: automatic sample-volume tracking would reduce how precisely a remote or novice operator has to hold the probe during Doppler.

Diagnostic performance of thoracic ultrasound and shear wave elastography in detecting malignant pleural effusion: meta-analysis

Msherghi A, Aissani MS, Leung AN, et al. · ERJ Open Res, 9 Nov 2026 · PubMed 43011977

The meta-analysis pooled 10 studies, three of them on SWE. B-mode signs such as pleural nodules were highly specific (up to 99%) but had sensitivity of 60% or less. SWE sensitivity ranged from 76% to 100% and specificity from 59% to 94% depending on the cut-off, and the authors say it needs more study.
