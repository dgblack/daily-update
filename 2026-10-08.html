Ultrasound Operator Guidance Using World Modeling and Retrieval Based Action Planning

Schueler, van Gorp, van Sloun (TU Eindhoven) · arXiv, 5 Oct 2026

A V-JEPA 2.1 encoder maps ultrasound frames into a latent space. The planner retrieves nearby latent states from a database that pairs those states with probe poses. It then reuses the probe motions actually executed from those states as a nonparametric transition model and plans over a receding horizon. Guidance uses only the live image, with no probe tracking. In retrospective carotid episodes it reached the target view 86% of the time, against 52% and 43% for two baselines. In a prospective test on unseen volunteers, running in real time on a CPU, it reached 83%. The same model can also bring the operator back to any earlier patient-specific frame.

Why it matters: tracker-free guidance toward a target view maps directly onto the expert-to-novice guidance in human teleoperation, and the return-to-a-saved-frame feature suits longitudinal tele-ultrasound.

UltraWorld: Learning Interactive Ultrasound World Models from Untracked Clinical Videos with Acoustic Sampling Map

Yang, Wang, Guan, Ren · arXiv (cs.CV/cs.RO), 7 Oct 2026

The authors adapt a video foundation model into an ultrasound generator conditioned on anatomical masks. They sample masks along programmed trajectories through 3D anatomy to create synthetic action-video pairs, and use these to distil an action-conditioned world model. At inference the model needs no masks or 3D assets. An “Acoustic Sampling Map” encodes probe pose and imaging settings as pixel-wise 3D sample positions, beam directions and depths. In nine simulated closed-loop planning episodes, it cut final goal distance by 29% and orientation error by 38% compared with visual servoing.

Why it matters: this is a way to get probe-motion models out of the large pool of untracked clinical video, relevant to autonomous scanning and to predictive displays for delayed teleoperation.

Data-Efficient Prostate Micro-Ultrasound Image Analysis using a Modality-specific Foundation Model (muFM)

Pang, Fan, Hung, …, Wodlinger (Exact Imaging), Kinnaird (Alberta), Brisbane, Sung (UCLA) · medRxiv, posted 5 Oct 2026

The model uses DINOv2-style self-supervised pretraining on 1.74 M unlabeled micro-ultrasound images from 780 patients. With only 10% of training scans, it reached capsule segmentation Dice of 93.8% (best comparator 87.9%) and urethra Dice of 58.4% (comparator 47.0%). For lesion localisation it reached about 0.75 sensitivity at 2 false positives per volume, beating foundation models pretrained on natural, medical and general ultrasound images. The pretraining data come from a single institution, and the model has no explicit 3D modelling.

Why it matters: it is a likely backbone for micro-ultrasound segmentation and registration, for example combined with shear-wave elastography or 3D reconstruction.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Beyond Retargeting: Low-Latency and Robust Humanoid Whole-Body Teleoperation with Learned Atomic Motion Primitives

Xu, Yu, Chen, Qian, Ma, Liu, Shi, Zhang · arXiv, 6 Oct 2026

A policy maps raw human motion straight to Unitree G1 joint commands in a single forward pass, with no online retargeting. A learned codebook of full-body motion primitives projects noisy, partial or out-of-distribution input onto plausible motions. It was tested with VR, optical mocap, text-to-motion and monocular-video inputs. The authors claim lower latency and better robustness than baselines, but the abstract gives no numbers.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

MRI-Free CT-to-Ultrasound Skull Registration in Transcranial Focused Ultrasound using CMUT-based Integrated Therapeutic and Imaging Arrays

Pakdaman Zangabad, Satyanarayana, Arvanitis, Degertekin (Georgia Tech) · arXiv (physics.med-ph), 4 Oct 2026

A hybrid CMUT array combines sparse therapy elements, used for pulse-echo skull localisation, with an imaging array that images the skull surface. Together they register a CT skull model without MRI. In Field II simulation over 125 realisations per setting, fused registration gave a median displacement error of 0.271 mm and a median rotation error of 0.90°.
