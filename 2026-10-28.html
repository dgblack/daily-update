AI-Driven Hyperspectral Imaging in Clinical Medicine: A Critical Appraisal of Translational Readiness

Meeran ABM, Chowdhury MEH, Naznine M, Hamid Md Ali S, Sankaran R, Pedersen S · Diagnostics 16(20):3338, online 14 Oct 2026 (indexed in PubMed this week) · PubMed

This PRISMA-ScR scoping review covers 130 AI-plus-HSI studies from 2015–2026. Surgical oncology (53%) and neurosurgery (10.8%) are the largest areas. None of the studies met the evidence criteria needed for regulatory approval, and 50.8% are still at proof of concept. The authors argue that the bottleneck is the lack of multicentre validation and of a regulatory evidence framework, not imaging performance.

Why it matters: it is a ready citation for the translational gap in HSI and fluorescence-guided brain-tumour work, and it argues for multicentre designs like Münster’s.

An Interactive Mobile Augmented Reality System for Efficient Virtual Model-to-Patient Registration in Thoracic Surgery

Emmanuel Ajaero, Pascal Spiegler, George Rakovich, Marta Kersten-Oertal, Yiming Xiao · arXiv (cs.HC), 27 Oct 2026

HILMARTS runs on a LiDAR iPad for VATS port planning. The user aligns the model coarsely by hand in AR, and point-cloud registration then refines it automatically. An occupancy-grid “Surface Coverage Score” tells the user live when the LiDAR scan is complete enough. In a phantom study (N = 16) it reached 3.31 ± 1.26 mm accuracy in about 4 min, with an SUS of 84.1.

Why it matters: combining a human coarse alignment with automatic refinement, guided by coverage feedback, is directly transferable to patient-model registration in MR tele-US.

Teleanatomy: A Conceptual Framework for Anatomy Across Distance

Iwanaga J, Tubbs RS · Clinical Anatomy, online 27 Oct 2026 · PubMed

The authors propose “teleanatomy” as the shared anatomical layer beneath telemedicine, remote surgery and digital anatomy. It rests on five pillars (acquisition, representation, transmission, interpretation, application) and three cross-cutting properties (fidelity, provenance, latency). The paper is conceptual and contains no experiments.

Why it matters: its vocabulary maps neatly onto patient modelling in model-mediated tele-US and could serve as framing in an introduction.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Prospective Clinical Evidence on AI-Enabled Augmented and Mixed Reality in Robot-Assisted Prostate and Kidney Cancer Surgery: A Systematic Review

Harrison G · Cureus 18(9):e116810, online 23 Sep 2026 (indexed in PubMed this week) · PubMed

Only 6 of 131 screened records were prospective studies. In robot-assisted radical prostatectomy (RARP), AI-AR guidance gave promising signals for margins and continence. In partial nephrectomy, MR improved warm ischaemia time and clamping strategy. The evidence base is thin and the review has a single author, so read it as a map of the literature rather than as evidence.

Computer vision & imaging

UT-HMR: Uncertainty-Guided Temporal Modeling for Robust 3D Human Mesh Recovery

Zhibo Wang, Sirisha Rambhatla, Yuhao Chen, Maria Koshkina · arXiv, 26 Oct 2026

This is a one-stage monocular HMR method. It models correlated pose uncertainty across joints and fuses evidence from neighbouring frames according to that uncertainty. It reports state-of-the-art results on ice-hockey and soccer benchmarks.

Why it matters: per-joint uncertainty could serve as a confidence gate when body-mesh fits drive patient models or probe-pose targets in tele-US.
