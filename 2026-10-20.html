SonoWorld: Towards Interactive Pose-Controllable Ultrasound World Models

Zefan Yang, Zheyuan Zhang, Boris Maihe, …, Terrence Chen, Shanhui Sun · arXiv (cs.CV), 14 Oct 2026

SonoWorld is a causal video-diffusion world model that generates the B-mode response to probe motion. It is trained in three stages:

  • pretraining with latent actions on about 6.9M unlabeled multi-organ frames;
  • grounding to metric 6-DoF poses using a small EM-tracked freehand thyroid dataset;
  • distillation for long-horizon autoregressive rollout.

It ranks the target-directed probe motion above the equal and opposite motion in up to 74.8% of trials. The authors call it the first pose-controllable ultrasound world model learned mainly from freehand clinical video.

Why it matters: a model like this could stand in for the patient on the remote side of model-mediated tele-ultrasound, predicting images during latency. It is also a simulator for training scanning policies.

K-AoI: A Spatio-Temporal Synchronization Metric for Digital Twin-based Teleoperation in Dynamic Network Environments

Lu Tian, Zhenhui Yuan, Zexin Deng, Wenjuan Zhang · arXiv (cs.RO/cs.NI), 19 Oct 2026 · IEEE SWC 2026 short paper

K-AoI weights age-of-information by the robot’s instantaneous Cartesian velocity, which turns staleness into a spatial divergence between the twin and the robot. The testbed is a 6-DOF arm with a Unity twin and tc netem impairments across seven network types. Rank correlation with plain AoI was at most 0.65, and only 0.22 over satellite. AoI tails did not fit the usual Generalized Pareto model. The dataset and code are on GitHub.

Why it matters: it is a simple, physically meaningful way to report network quality in MR or digital-twin tele-ultrasound papers, in mm of probe divergence rather than ms.

DinoMUSE: A DINOv2-guided Framework for Unsupervised Lateral Motion Estimation in Ultrasound Elastography

Xiyao Meng, Xingyue Wei, Baihua Luo, Bo Peng, Jianwen Luo · arXiv (eess.IV), 16 Oct 2026

DinoMUSE fuses DINOv2 global features into an unsupervised displacement network through a mixture-of-experts module. The aim is to fix lateral displacement, which is the weak axis in quasi-static elastography. It reports lower lateral displacement and strain error in simulation, and better strain SNR and CNR on in-vivo breast data.

Why it matters: better lateral estimates help strain imaging and 2D/3D shear-wave motion tracking, which could carry over to prostate micro-ultrasound.

Remote Expert Evaluation of Tabletop Avatar Gaze Control in Asymmetric Multi-Scale MR–VR Collaboration

Carlos Mosquera, Neven Elsayed, Ernst Kruijff, Joseph Newman, Valeriya Lehrbaum, Eduardo Veas · arXiv (cs.HC), 19 Oct 2026 · ACM VRST 2026

A remote expert in MR moves a tangible avatar within a world-in-miniature of an industrial plant. Inside the miniature, the avatar shares the on-site operator’s scale and egocentric view. The study compared three gaze-control modes with 29 participants on same-machine and twin-machine fault-detection tasks: immersive VR, a floating head-controlled screen and an on-avatar touchpad. Immersive VR with direct head control performed best and gave the best user experience.

Why it matters: it is closely analogous to the expert–follower roles in human teleoperation, and it supports giving the remote expert an egocentric view rather than only an overview.

Teleoperation & mixed reality

Intent-Informed Model Predictive Control for Shared Autonomy in Remote Manipulation Tasks

Lorena Maria Genua, Camilla Tabasso, Mya Schroder, et al. · arXiv (cs.RO), 18 Oct 2026

A goal belief is inferred from operator gaze and arm motion. The entropy of that belief sets the level of autonomy, which is blended into an MPC. Compared with baselines, retrieval time fell by at least 61% and operator input by 60%.

Scalable, Intuitive, Mixed Reality-Based Semi-Autonomous Robotic Telemanipulation for the Execution of a Fine-Grained Bimanual Task

Shifei Duan, Zhe Wang, Francesco De Pace, Minas Liarokapis · arXiv, 18 Oct 2026 · IROS 2026

An MR headset lets the operator supervise a robot that executes most of each trajectory itself. The operator only makes fine corrections at key stages of a bimanual peg-in-hole task. The demonstrations collected this way trained imitation policies with less operator effort. The abstract gives no numbers.

Evaluating Lightweight Contact Feedback in Dual-Operator Collaborative Humanoid Teleoperation

Austin Graves, Chuizheng Kong, Yunho Cho, et al. · arXiv (cs.RO), 16 Oct 2026

Fingertip contact was fed back as vibrotactile or auditory cues in a 16-dyad, two-humanoid study. Operators rated vibrotactile cues as more effective, but differences in task performance were not resolved.

FOMI: A Force-Feedback Manipulation Interface for Teaching Contact-Rich Manipulation

Yishu Shen, Yiming Luo, Ruilin Wang, et al. · arXiv (cs.RO), 14 Oct 2026

FOMI is a handheld, scissor-shaped interface that passes contact forces to the operator’s hands without force sensors. Collection efficiency was about 29% better than the best baseline for robot-free collection and about 18% better for teleoperation.

Perceptual Comparisons of Viscoelastic Renderings: Fractional- vs Integer-Order Models

Gorkem Gemalmaz, Harun Tolasa, Volkan Patoglu · arXiv, 15 Oct 2026

In human-subject experiments, participants judged a fractional-order viscoelastic haptic model more realistic than two integer-order models. This held with both identification-based and perception-optimised parameters.

Why it matters: this is relevant to choosing tissue models for rendering patient contact in model-mediated tele-ultrasound.

V-DART: VR-Based Dual-Arm Robot Teleoperation with Mobile Base Control and Data Recording

A. Torrejón, J. Calderón, P. Núñez, P. Bustos · Sensors 26(19):6245, Oct 2026 · PMID 42884446

V-DART is an Unreal Engine 5 VR teleoperation framework for a mobile dual-arm robot. The base can be driven by joystick or by mapping the user’s physical position, and pose, input and robot state are logged for behaviour cloning. The abstract gives no quantitative results.

Analytical Kinematic Model and RCM Control of a Parallel-Serial Steady-Hand Eye Robot

B. Zhao, M. Esfandiari, …, P. Kazanzides, I. Iordachita · J Mech Robot 18(11), 2026 · PMID 42866388

The authors derive closed-form kinematics and Jacobians for SHER 3.0 and use MPC to enforce the remote centre of motion at the sclerotomy. In a pilot teleoperation study, RMS RCM error was 0.55 ± 0.12 mm.

Ultrasound & robotic ultrasound

Prospective Comparison of Cognitive MRI-Targeted Biopsy Under Micro-Ultrasound Guidance Versus MRI/Ultrasound Fusion-Guided Transperineal Biopsy for Clinically Significant Prostate Cancer Detection

A. Fraia, S. Riolo, F. Di Bello, et al. · J Clin Med 15(19):7649, 2 Oct 2026 · PMID 42882109

The study had 58 patients with PI-RADS ≥3 lesions, 29 per arm. Clinically significant cancer was detected in 37.9% of each arm. Overall detection was 65.5% with fusion and 55.2% with cognitive targeting under micro-ultrasound (p = 0.59). PSA density predicted csPCa, but technique did not. The authors say the sample is too small to claim equivalence.

Spatiotemporal Adaptive Eigenspectrum Remapping in Transcranial Ultrasound Localization Microscopy

Stephen A. Lee, Isidora Jelenkovic, Maia V. Kokoeva, Jean Provost · arXiv, 16 Oct 2026

The method replaces hard SVD clutter truncation with continuous eigenvalue remapping via ZCA whitening. It suppresses skull reverberation while keeping slow flow, and it improved microbubble contrast and track density in mouse brain.

Computer vision & imaging

Landmark-Free and Scale-Blind Multi-View Registration of Preoperative Liver Models to Intraoperative Surfaces

Jiaming Feng, Gerardo Loza Galindo, Shahid Farid, et al. · arXiv, 16 Oct 2026

SimCompose registers preoperative liver models to intraoperative surfaces for AR laparoscopy, with no landmarks or metric scale. Multi-view fusion raised the success rate on synthetic data to 77% and reduced surface error in in-vivo patients.

Design and Characterization of a Unified Continuum Robot Actuation Platform

Yunti Xu, Aedan Mangan, Miheer Potdar, et al. · arXiv (cs.RO), 14 Oct 2026

This is an open-source, modular actuation and ROS stack that is decoupled from any particular continuum-robot design. It achieves sub-millimetre translational accuracy and is shown in two surgical use cases.
